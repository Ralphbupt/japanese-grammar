## 2026-11-22 · 历年真题语法、JLPT 估分器||2026-11-22 · Grammar from past tests, JLPT score calculator

:::zh
新页面「历年真题语法」：我们逐场看了真题的语法题，统计每个语法考过几次、哪几场，按次数排序，写明接续、意思，并链接到讲解。[N1](/jlpt-n1-past-exam-grammar/)（32 场，294 个语法）、[N2](/jlpt-n2-past-exam-grammar/)（32 场，247 个）、[N3](/jlpt-n3-past-exam-grammar/)（31 场，145 个）是 2010–2026 年的全部场次；[N4](/jlpt-n4-past-exam-grammar/)（10 场）和 [N5](/jlpt-n5-past-exam-grammar/)（7 场）只收录了我们找到的场次。每一级都有差不多一半考的是低一级的语法。课文里考过的语法点下面会标出各级真题考过几次，练习题答完、语法听读页上也有同样的标记。

新页面 [我的生词本](/words/)：全站加入的单词都在一页，按 N5–N1 分开，可以只背某一级；顶栏有入口。新页面 [我的语法卡](/my-grammar/)：课文里点「＋ 语法卡」加入的语法点，加上练习里做错的语法点，按级别用卡片背，进度和语法卡片页相通。语法听读页可以选「只读日语」或「只读例句」。

课文里的例句和「本课单词表」的词都可以点了：看读音和意思，一键加入生词本，和练习页、语法听读页是同一个生词本。语法听读页的「真题考过」标签现在列出各级别的次数和具体场次。

每个语法点可以标「已掌握」：课文里每个语法点下面、真题语法页每一行都有按钮，和[语法清单](/N5_grammar_list/)的勾是同一个状态，登录后会同步；真题语法页可以只看还没掌握的，做题分析会提醒标了已掌握却又做错的语法点。

新页面 [JLPT 估分器](/jlpt-score-calculator/)：选级别，填每部分大概答对几道，就能看到各科估分、总分，以及有没有过总分线和单科线。[12 月考前指南](/jlpt-december-2026/) 加了最后一周的安排。
:::

:::en
New pages: grammar from past tests. We went through the grammar items of real JLPT papers and counted how often each pattern came up and when, most-tested first, each with its form, meaning and a link to its lesson: [N1](/jlpt-n1-past-exam-grammar/) (32 tests, 294 patterns), [N2](/jlpt-n2-past-exam-grammar/) (32 tests, 247) and [N3](/jlpt-n3-past-exam-grammar/) (31 tests, 145) cover every sitting from 2010 to 2026; [N4](/jlpt-n4-past-exam-grammar/) (10 tests) and [N5](/jlpt-n5-past-exam-grammar/) (7 tests) only the ones we found. At every level about half the patterns are grammar from a lower level. Lessons now mark how often each level's tests asked a point, and so do practice questions (after you answer) and the grammar audio pages.

New page: [My word list](/words/) — every word you saved anywhere on the site, sorted N5 to N1, with flashcards for all of them or one level; it's in the top bar. New page: [My grammar cards](/my-grammar/) — the points you add from the lessons (“＋ Grammar card”) plus the ones you miss in practice, by level, as flashcards that share progress with the flashcard pages. The grammar audio pages can read the Japanese only, or the examples only.

Words in the lessons' example sentences and vocabulary tables are tappable now: reading and meaning, and one tap adds a word to your word list — the same list as the practice and grammar audio pages. The grammar audio pages' past-test tags now show every level and the sittings.

Every grammar point can be marked "got it": there's a button under each point in the lessons and on each row of the past-test pages, sharing one state with the [grammar checklists](/N5_grammar_list/) and synced when you're signed in. The past-test pages can hide the points you know, and the practice analysis flags points you marked but missed afterwards.

New page: [JLPT score calculator](/jlpt-score-calculator/). Pick a level, enter roughly how many you got right in each part, and see your estimated section scores, total, and whether you clear the pass mark and every section minimum. The [December exam guide](/jlpt-december-2026/) gained a final-week plan.
:::

:::en
New page: the [N2 vocabulary list](/n2-vocabulary-list/) — the ~700 words the N2 vocabulary section has tested since 2010, grouped by how often they came up, each with reading, meaning, an example and its translation, all read aloud as one track. Start with “Quick sort”, one card per word: the ones you don’t know go into your word list, the ones you know drop out. Then turn on “Only my words” and the player reads just those. Tap any word in an example (on the grammar audio pages too) for its reading and meaning, and to save it.

The same list now exists for [N5](/n5-vocabulary-list/) (183 words) and [N4](/n4-vocabulary-list/) (274 words); N3 and N1 follow shortly.

## 2026-11-21 · 对照历年真题校准题库||2026-11-21 · Question bank checked against past papers

:::zh
我们把 2010–2026 年的 JLPT 真题（N1–N5 共 100 多回）逐题整理，和本站的课程、题库做了一次全面对照，据此做了这些调整：

- **模拟考按新版题量**：2020 年代 JLPT 调整过 N1/N2 的题量。N1、N2 模拟考改成现在的结构：中文（内容理解・中文）为 4 篇 × 2 题，N2 語形成 3 题、即時応答 11 题，文章の文法 4 个空，统合理解等也按新版删减。N3 文章の文法也改为 4 个空。N1、N2 每套模拟考各新写了一篇中文。
- **阅读文章加长到真题长度**：本站的阅读文章原来普遍比真题短 15–20%。这次把明显偏短的 47 篇（N5–N1 的中文、文章の文法、統合理解等）加长到真题的长度，题目和答案不变。
- **补上真题常考、课程里没单独讲的语法**：N2 新增 〜に欠かせない、〜たところ（～了一下，结果…）、〜そうになる、〜がたい、〜を込めて、〜に次いで、〜と思うと、〜とされている、〜というように，并在已有语法点里补充 〜とのことだ、〜でしかない；N4 敬语课补充 お越しくださる / おいでくださる / お見えになる、お〜です、お〜いただく / ご覧いただく、承る。
  其他级别同样补齐：N1 新增 15 个（いっさい／なんら〜ない、〜かというと〜ない、〜ない限り、〜かと思いきや、〜てこそ、〜てまで、〜にあって、〜にもほどがある 等），N3 新增 10 个（〜てもおかしくない、〜のほかに、少しも〜ない、〜という点で、〜てみると、〜直す 等），N4 新增 〜ずつ、〜たまま、〜なくなる、〜中、〜と同じ、〜のに（用途），N5 补充 人数・方式的「で」和频率的「に」。每个点都配了练习题，[语法音频](/grammar-audio/)也已收录。
- **词汇题换成真题常考词**：约 190 道词汇练习题换了考点——原来考的是低于本级或重复的词，现在换成真题反复出现、而题库里还没有的词（如 N2 略す・湿る・違反・尽きる，N1 承諾・満喫・ことごとく）。生词本释义同步补齐。
- **新专题：[日语接续词（N5–N1）](/japanese-conjunctions/)**：だから・それで・そこで・すると・実は……按级别整理，文章の文法常考。
:::

:::en
We worked through the 2010–2026 JLPT papers (over 100 sittings, N1–N5) question by question and compared them with the lessons and the question bank. What changed:

- **Mock tests follow the current format.** The JLPT trimmed N1/N2 in the 2020s. Our N1 and N2 mocks now match: 4 mid-length reading passages × 2 questions, N2 word formation 3 items and quick response 11, 4 blanks in text grammar, and fewer integrated-comprehension items. N3 text grammar is down to 4 blanks too. Each N1 and N2 mock got one new mid-length passage.
- **Reading passages at real length.** Our passages ran 15–20% shorter than the real test. The 47 clearly short ones (mid-length reading, text grammar, integrated comprehension at N5–N1) are now as long as the real thing; questions and answers are unchanged.
- **Grammar the test asks but our lessons didn't teach.** New N2 points: 〜に欠かせない, 〜たところ (did X and found…), 〜そうになる, 〜がたい, 〜を込めて, 〜に次いで, 〜と思うと, 〜とされている, 〜というように; 〜とのことだ and 〜でしかない added to existing points. The N4 keigo lessons now cover お越しくださる / おいでくださる / お見えになる, お〜です, お〜いただく / ご覧いただく and 承る.
  The other levels got the same treatment: 15 new N1 points (いっさい／なんら〜ない, 〜かというと〜ない, 〜ない限り, 〜かと思いきや, 〜てこそ, 〜てまで, 〜にあって, 〜にもほどがある …), 10 new N3 points (〜てもおかしくない, 〜のほかに, 少しも〜ない, 〜という点で, 〜てみると, 〜直す …), N4 〜ずつ, 〜たまま, 〜なくなる, 〜中, 〜と同じ and 〜のに (purpose), and at N5 で for "by oneself / together" and に for frequency. Each comes with practice questions and is in the [grammar audio](/grammar-audio/).
- **Vocabulary questions on the words the test keeps asking.** About 190 vocabulary practice questions now test a different word: ones that tested a word below the level or a duplicate were rewritten around words the real papers use again and again that our bank didn't have yet (N2 略す, 湿る, 違反, 尽きる; N1 承諾, 満喫, ことごとく …). The word list has meanings for them.
- **New guide: [Japanese conjunctions, N5–N1](/japanese-conjunctions/)** — だから, それで, そこで, すると, 実は … by level, the words text-grammar questions hinge on.
:::
