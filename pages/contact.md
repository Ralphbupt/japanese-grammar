# 联系我们||Contact

:::zh
jpnotes.dev 由一个人维护：一个自己也在准备 JLPT 的日语学习者（见[关于](/about/)）。
:::

:::en
jpnotes.dev is run by one person: a Japanese learner preparing for the JLPT too (see [About](/about/)).
:::

## 怎么联系||How to reach me

:::zh
- **邮件**：hello@jpnotes.dev。适合任何事情：问题、建议、合作、转载、数据删除请求。一般几天内回复。
- **反馈框**：每页底部的「💬 反馈」，或练习题下的「这题有问题？」。不用登录；留下邮箱的话，回复会同时发到你的邮箱。
- **GitHub**：发现课文或代码的错误，也可以在 [GitHub](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/issues) 提 issue。
:::

:::en
- **Email**: hello@jpnotes.dev — for anything: questions, ideas, partnerships, reuse, requests to delete your data. Replies usually come within a few days.
- **Feedback box**: "💬 Feedback" at the foot of every page, or "Something wrong with this question?" under a practice question. No account needed; leave an email and the reply is mailed to you too.
- **GitHub**: mistakes in the lessons or the code can also go to [GitHub issues](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/issues).
:::

## 报告错误时||When reporting a mistake

:::zh
请写上页面（或题号）和哪一句，有参考资料更好。确认后会尽快改正，并在[更新日志](/changelog/)里记下来。
:::

:::en
Say which page (or question) and which sentence; a source helps. Confirmed mistakes are fixed quickly and noted in the [changelog](/changelog/).
:::
