# 关于本站||About

## 这是什么||What is this

:::zh
jpnotes.dev 是一份免费、双语（中文 + 英文）的 JLPT N5 → N1 语法讲解和题库，共 **{{LESSONS}} 课、{{POINTS}}+ 个语法点**。每个语法点包含：

- **接续规则**：动词 / 形容词 / 名词的具体接续方式
- **3+ 例句**：覆盖日常对话和考试常见语境
- **辨析**：和易混淆语法的对比（如「は」vs「が」、「たら」vs「なら」）
- **易错点**：中文母语者特别容易踩的坑
- **间隔复习**：当天 → 1 → 4 → 7 → 14 → 30 天勾选清单

内容免费、无广告。访问量只用 Google Analytics 做匿名统计。
:::

:::en
jpnotes.dev is a free JLPT N5 → N1 grammar reference and question bank, with every explanation written in both English and Chinese: **{{LESSONS}} lessons covering {{POINTS}}+ grammar points**. Each grammar point includes:

- **Conjugation rules**: how it combines with verbs / adjectives / nouns
- **3+ example sentences**: covering daily conversation and exam-style contexts
- **Comparisons**: side-by-side with easily confused grammar (e.g. は vs. が, たら vs. なら)
- **Common pitfalls**: mistakes Chinese native speakers tend to make
- **Spaced repetition**: review checklist for day 0 → 1 → 4 → 7 → 14 → 30

Free and ad-free. Visits are counted anonymously with Google Analytics.

## 为什么做这个||Why I built it

:::zh
我从 2026 年 4 月开始系统学日语，目标 8 周内拿下 JLPT N2。准备过程中发现：

- 中文圈的日语语法资料要么过于碎片化（散落在各种论坛、知乎回答），要么过于学院派
- 教科书（《大家的日本语》《新完全マスター》等）对中文母语者来说，「中文翻译」和「中文讲解」之间有断层——很多语感差异翻译里讲不清楚
- 没有一份完整覆盖 N5 → N2、又用**中文母语者视角**讲解的免费在线资料

所以我决定**边学边记**，把整套笔记开源出来。最初是给自己复习用，后来发现可能对其他中文母语日语学习者也有用，于是托管成网站。
:::

:::en
I started studying Japanese seriously in April 2026, aiming for JLPT N2 in 8 weeks. While preparing, I noticed:

- Chinese-language Japanese grammar material online is either too fragmented (scattered across forums and Q&A sites) or too academic
- Textbooks (*Minna no Nihongo*, *Shin Kanzen Master*) leave a gap between "Chinese translation" and "explanation tuned for Chinese speakers" — much of the nuance gets lost
- No single resource covers N5 → N2 completely while explaining from a **Chinese-native-speaker perspective**

So I decided to **take notes as I learn** and open-source everything. It started as a personal study aid; publishing it as a website was a side effect.
:::

## 内容生产方式||How content is produced

:::zh
每节课的笔记结构来自我自己学日语的实际节奏。例句和辨析参考了：

- **教科书**：《大家的日本语 初级 I / II》、《新完全マスター 文法 N3 / N2》、《日本語総まとめ N3 / N2 文法》
- **在线参考**：[沪江日语](https://jp.hjenglish.com/)、[Bunpro](https://bunpro.jp/)、[Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/)、[日本語教師の広場](https://nihongokyoshi-net.com/)
- **真题**：JLPT 历年 N5 - N2 文法真题（公开发布部分）

各部分的来源和检查方式不同：

- **N5–N2 语法课（第 0–75 课）**：2026 年 4–6 月边学边写。每个语法点在我自己的学习中先验证 **2-3 周**（用配套的间隔复习清单），确认讲解清晰、例句到位之后再公开发布。
- **N1 语法课（第 76–92 课）**：2026 年 10 月由 AI 编写，再由 AI 另做一轮审校。我自己还没学到 N1，这部分没有经过上面那样的亲自验证，使用时请多留个心。
- **练习题、模拟题、听力题**：原创题，按 JLPT 的题型编写，**不是历年真题**。由 AI 辅助编写；每道题都做过「盲答」检查（先不看答案作答，答不出唯一正确答案的题会被改写），并经过自动校验。
- **音频**：例句朗读用微软 Edge 的语音合成，听力题用 AI 语音合成，语速略慢、发音清晰，不是考试录音。读音都用语音识别转写回文字核对过。

> 发现讲解、例句、题目或读音有错，请点题目下的「这题有问题？」或提 issue，我会修正。

> 如果你发现某个例句直接来自上述资料而我没标注出处，请提 issue，我会立刻修正。
:::

:::en
The lesson structure follows my actual study pace. Example sentences and comparisons reference:

- **Textbooks**: *Minna no Nihongo I/II*, *Shin Kanzen Master Bunpou N3/N2*, *Nihongo Sou Matome N3/N2 Bunpou*
- **Online**: [Hujiang Japanese](https://jp.hjenglish.com/), [Bunpro](https://bunpro.jp/), [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/), [Nihongo Kyoushi no Hiroba](https://nihongokyoshi-net.com/)
- **Past exams**: publicly released JLPT N5–N2 grammar questions

Different parts of the site were made and checked differently:

- **N5–N2 grammar lessons (0–75)**: written while I studied, April–June 2026. Each grammar point is validated through **2–3 weeks** of my own study (using the built-in spaced-repetition checklist) before being published.
- **N1 grammar lessons (76–92)**: written by AI in October 2026 and checked by a second AI review. I haven't studied N1 myself yet, so these lessons have not had that first-hand check; use them with a little more caution.
- **Practice questions, mock tests and listening**: original questions in the JLPT formats, **not past exam papers**. Written with AI assistance; every question went through a blind check (answered without seeing the key; any question without exactly one defensible answer was rewritten) and automated validation.
- **Audio**: example sentences are read by Microsoft Edge text-to-speech, and listening questions use AI voices, slightly slow and clearly pronounced, not exam recordings. Readings were checked by transcribing the audio back to text.

> Found a mistake in an explanation, example, question or reading? Use "Something wrong with this question?" under the question, or file an issue, and I'll fix it.

## 「问 AI」怎么用||How "Ask AI" works

:::zh
课程页右上角的 🤖 按钮会打开一个侧栏，AI 已经读过这一课，可以直接问；点任意例句后面的「讲解」，它会简明地拆解这句的语法、给出翻译和易错点；在正文里选中一段文字再点「💬 问 AI」，会把这段放进输入框，问什么由你自己写。练习题和模拟题答完后的「🤖 AI 讲解」用的是同一个侧栏。

**打开就能用**：默认使用本站提供的免费模型（Anthropic 的 Claude Haiku 5.5），不用注册、不用 key，每人每天最多 50 次（全站共用一份每日额度）。在中国大陆、香港、澳门等 Claude 不支持的地区，以及额度用完或出错时，由备用模型回答。AI 的回答可能有错，以课文和解析为准。

输入框上方的 📎 决定随问题发给 AI 的范围：默认「跟随阅读位置」，只发送你正在看的那一节；也可以选整课或某一节。
:::

:::en
The 🤖 button on every lesson page opens a side panel; the AI has already read the lesson, so just ask. Click "Explain" after any example sentence for a short grammar breakdown with translation and pitfalls, or select any text and click "💬 Ask AI" to put it in the input box and ask your own question. "🤖 AI explanation" after a practice or mock test question uses the same panel.

**It works out of the box**: by default it uses a free model this site provides (Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5), with no sign-up and no key, up to 50 questions per reader per day (the whole site shares one daily allowance). In regions Claude does not support, or when the allowance runs out or the model fails, a backup model answers. AI answers can be wrong, so trust the notes and explanations first.

The 📎 selector above the input sets what is sent with your question: by default "follow my reading", only the grammar point on screen; you can also pick the whole lesson or one section.
:::

## 「问 AI」功能的隐私||"Ask AI" privacy

:::zh
「问 AI」把当前课的内容连同你的问题发给一个大模型。它的设计原则是：**本站不保存你的问题和回答。**

- **回答经过本站中转。** 由一个 Cloudflare Worker（ai.jpnotes.dev）把你的问题和这一课的正文交给 Anthropic 的 Claude，再把回答传回来。如果你所在地区 Claude 不支持，或当天额度用完、出错，会改由 Cloudflare Workers AI 上的模型回答，再不行就由 Groq 或 Cerebras 上的模型回答：都只转发问题和这一课的正文，不附带你的 IP，由这些服务商按各自的隐私政策处理。本站不记录问题、回答，也不开启请求日志；为了每天限次，它只保存 IP 的加盐哈希，按天计数，两天后自动删除；另外只统计每次请求来自哪个按钮（讲解、快捷提问等）和消耗了多少额度，不含内容和 IP。[中转源码](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/blob/main/worker/src/index.js)同样公开。
- **对话只在你的浏览器里。** 每一课的对话记录、练习题的讲解记录都存在这台设备的浏览器里（localStorage），不上传，也不随账号同步；点「清空对话」即删除。
- **浏览器层面的白名单。** 每个页面都带有 Content-Security-Policy 的 `connect-src` 规则，只允许连接本站自己的服务（AI 中转、账号同步）、Google Analytics 和 Google 登录，以及开发用的 localhost。即使页面上某段脚本被篡改，浏览器也会拒绝把数据发往白名单之外的任何地址。
- **自行验证。** 按 F12 打开开发者工具的 Network 面板，再提一个问题：你只会看到发往 ai.jpnotes.dev 的请求。侧栏的[全部源码](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/blob/main/site/ai-assistant.js)只有一个文件，欢迎审阅。

「问 AI」免费，额度由本站承担。早期版本可以填自己的 API key 直连其他服务商；因为没有人使用，这个功能已于 2026 年 11 月移除，之前保存在浏览器里的密钥会在下次打开侧栏时自动清除。
:::

:::en
"Ask AI" sends the current lesson plus your question to a large language model. Its design rule: **this site does not store your questions or the answers.**

- **Answers go through this site's relay.** A Cloudflare Worker (ai.jpnotes.dev) that passes your question and the lesson text to Anthropic's Claude and streams the answer back. If Claude isn't supported in your region, or the day's allowance is used up, or it fails, a model on Cloudflare Workers AI answers instead, then one on Groq or Cerebras: in every case only the question and the lesson text are sent, not your IP, and those providers handle the request under their own privacy policies. The relay does not log questions or answers and has request logging turned off; to enforce the daily limit it keeps only a salted hash of your IP, counted per day and deleted after two days. Beyond that it only counts which button each request came from (Explain, quick prompts, …) and how much quota it used — no text, no IP. The [relay source](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/blob/main/worker/src/index.js) is public too.
- **Conversations stay in your browser.** Each lesson's chat and the log of practice explanations are kept in this device's browser (localStorage); they are not uploaded and an account does not sync them. "Clear chat" deletes them.
- **A browser-enforced allowlist.** Every page ships a Content-Security-Policy `connect-src` rule that only permits connections to this site's own services (the AI relay, account sync), Google Analytics and Google sign-in, plus localhost for development. Even if a script on the page were tampered with, the browser would refuse to send data anywhere else.
- **Verify it yourself.** Open DevTools (F12) → Network and ask a question: the only requests you will see go to ai.jpnotes.dev. The panel's [entire source](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/blob/main/site/ai-assistant.js) is one file; reviews welcome.

"Ask AI" is free; this site covers the cost. Earlier versions let you add your own API key and talk to other providers directly; nobody used it, so it was removed in November 2026, and a key saved in your browser is deleted the next time you open the panel.
:::

## 登录与进度同步的隐私||Sign-in and progress sync: what is stored

:::zh
右上角的账号按钮可以用 Google 账号或邮箱验证码登录（测试中），登录后做题进度会在你的各个设备之间同步。**登录完全可选：不登录时，进度只存在这个浏览器里，本站的服务器收不到任何东西。**

- **保存什么。** 你的邮箱地址；注册时所在的国家或地区（Cloudflare 根据网络给出的两个字母代码，不是 IP 地址）；用 Google 登录时，Google 给这个账号的一串编号（用来认出下次登录的是同一个人）；登录会话（只保存令牌的哈希，180 天后过期）；以及你的做题进度：每题最近几次作答、错题本、每天做了多少题、模拟考成绩和没考完的那一场、语法卡片的复习状态、练习页的几项偏好（每组题数、听力语速等）。为了能恢复到之前的状态（账号面板里的「恢复到之前的版本」），还会保留这份进度最近几次同步前的版本，以及过去 14 天每天最后一份。
- **不保存什么。** 姓名、头像（Google 会一并发来，本站直接丢弃）；密码（Google 从不把它交给任何网站）；「问 AI」的对话；IP 地址。这个服务没有开启请求日志。
- **存在哪里。** 本站自己的 Cloudflare 数据库（D1，亚太区），只有 api.jpnotes.dev 上的一个 Cloudflare Worker 能写入，它的[源码](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/blob/main/worker/src/account.js)公开。除此之外只有站长自己的统计页会读取，而且只显示数量（账号数、每天注册数、国家、同步次数），不显示邮箱，也不显示进度内容。合并两台设备进度的规则在[这个文件](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/blob/main/site/sync.js)里。
- **用来做什么。** 只用来同步进度。不会给你发推广邮件，不会把邮箱或进度交给任何第三方。
- **邮箱验证码。** 选择用邮箱登录时，6 位验证码由发信服务 Resend 代发，所以它会经手你的邮箱地址和这封邮件；验证码本身只以哈希形式保存 10 分钟。为了防止被人拿来乱发邮件，还会保存一个按日期加盐的 IP 哈希，用来限制每天的发送次数，隔天清除。
- **Google 能看到什么。** 登录按钮来自 Google 的脚本，只在登录框出现时才加载；Google 会知道你用这个账号登录过 jpnotes.dev，但看不到你的做题进度。
- **这个浏览器里。** 登录令牌存在 localStorage；第一次和云端合并之前，会在本机留一份合并前的进度备份。
- **退出和删除。** 「退出登录」只结束这台设备的会话，本机和云端的进度都保留。账号面板里还可以导出、导入进度，按级别或全部清空（清空会同步到你的所有设备）。「删除账号和云端数据」会立刻删掉上面列出的全部内容，本机浏览器里的进度不受影响；Cloudflare 的数据库备份最多再保留 30 天后自动清除。
:::

:::en
The account button (top right) lets you sign in with a Google account or a code sent to your email (in beta) so your progress follows you across devices. **Signing in is optional: without it, progress stays in this browser and this site's server receives nothing.**

- **What is stored.** Your email address; the country or region you signed up from (the two-letter code Cloudflare gives for the network, not your IP address); with Google sign-in, the id number Google assigns to the account (to recognise the same person next time); your sign-in sessions (only a hash of the token, expiring after 180 days); and your progress: the last few answers per question, the review list, how many questions you did each day, mock test results and an unfinished test, flashcard review state, and a few practice settings (set size, listening speed and the like). Earlier copies are kept as well, so you can go back ("Restore an earlier copy" in the account panel): the copies before your last few syncs, and the last one of each of the past 14 days.
- **What is not stored.** Your name and picture (Google sends them along; they are discarded); a password (Google never gives one to any site); "Ask AI" conversations; IP addresses. Request logging is turned off for this service.
- **Where.** In this site's own Cloudflare database (D1, Asia-Pacific), written only by one Cloudflare Worker at api.jpnotes.dev, whose [source](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/blob/main/worker/src/account.js) is public. The only other reader is the site owner's private stats page, which shows counts (accounts, sign-ups per day, countries, number of syncs), never email addresses or the contents of anyone's progress. The rules for merging two devices' progress are in [this file](https://github.com/Ralphbupt/japanese-grammar/blob/main/site/sync.js).
- **What it is used for.** Syncing your progress, nothing else. No marketing email, and neither your address nor your progress is passed to anyone.
- **Email codes.** If you sign in by email, the 6-digit code is sent through the mail service Resend, which therefore handles your address and that one message; the code itself is kept only as a hash, for 10 minutes. To stop the form being used to send mail at random, a hash of your IP salted with the date is kept to cap sends per day, and cleared the next day.
- **What Google sees.** The sign-in button comes from Google's script, loaded only when the sign-in box is shown. Google learns that you signed in to jpnotes.dev with that account; it does not see your progress.
- **In this browser.** The sign-in token is kept in localStorage, and just before the first merge with the online copy a backup of your progress as it was is saved locally.
- **Signing out and deleting.** "Sign out" ends the session on this device only; progress stays both here and online. The account panel also lets you export and import your progress and clear it, one level or all of it (clearing reaches all your devices). "Delete account and synced data" removes everything listed above at once and leaves the progress in this browser alone; Cloudflare's database backups expire on their own within 30 days.
:::
