# JLPT N1 历年真题语法||JLPT N1 Grammar from Past Tests (2010–2026)

:::zh
本站逐场统计了 2010–2026 年 32 场 JLPT N1 真题的语法题，一共考过 **294 个语法**。下面按考过的次数排列，每个都写了接续、意思、考过哪几次，并链接到本站的讲解。其中 **43 个考过 3 次以上**，最值得先看；而且有 **133 个其实是 N2 及以下的语法**——N1 并不只考 N1 语法，N2 的基础不牢，语法题照样丢分。

用法建议：先把「考过 3 次以上」的过一遍，点进讲解看例句；再到 [N1 练习题](/n1-practice/) 做题，题目考到的语法如果在真题里出现过，答题后会标出「真题考过几次」。
:::
:::en
We went through the grammar items of all 32 real JLPT N1 tests from 2010 to 2026: **294 grammar patterns** came up. They're listed by how often each was tested, with its form, meaning, the sittings it appeared in, and a link to the lesson that teaches it. **43 came up three times or more** — start there. And **133 are N2-or-lower grammar**: N1 doesn't only test N1 patterns, so gaps in N2 cost points too.

How to use it: go through the patterns tested 3+ times first and open their lessons for examples; then drill in [N1 practice](/n1-practice/) — a question on a pattern from this list shows how often the real test asked it.
:::
