# JLPT 做题技巧（N5–N1）：哪些技巧在真题里真的灵||JLPT Test Tips (N5–N1) — Which Tricks Actually Work on Past Papers

:::zh
考前最后几周，到处都是"做题技巧"：道歉的选项要选、反问句的选项不选、带「必ず・全て」的选项大多是错的、照搬原文的选项不选、"最后定下来的才是答案"……这些说法有的很灵，有的在真题里根本不成立，还有的**只在某些级别灵**。这一页把最常听到的技巧整理在一起，**每一条都拿 2010–2026 年 N5–N1 的真题逐级核对过**（N1、N2 各 32 次考试，N3 30 次，N4 12 次，N5 9 次）：带这个特征的选项，真正是正解的比例是多少，和瞎猜相比是好是坏。页面里只给统计数字，不引用真题原文；示例句都是本站新写的。

用法：先看下面的总表，找到你要考的那一列，知道哪些技巧可以放心用、哪些要丢掉；再按部分（排序、阅读、听力）看具体做法；最后是"实在不会时猜哪个"。
:::
:::en
In the last weeks before the test, "exam tricks" are everywhere: always pick the apology, never pick the answer that asks a question back, options with 必ず or 全て are usually wrong, options that copy the passage are traps, "whatever gets settled last is the answer"… Some of these work well, some simply don't hold on real papers, and some **only work at certain levels**. This page collects the tips you hear most and **checks each one, level by level, against the 2010–2026 N5–N1 past papers** (32 sittings each for N1 and N2, 30 for N3, 12 for N4, 9 for N5): when an option has the feature, how often is it really the answer — better or worse than a blind guess? Only the counts are shown, no past-paper text; every example sentence here is our own.

How to use it: start with the summary table, find the column for your level and see which tricks to keep and which to drop, then read the technique for each part (sentence order, reading, listening), and finally what to pick when you have to guess.
:::

## 怎么读这些数字||How to Read the Numbers

:::zh
- **技巧是用来排除的，不是用来代替理解的。** N2 道歉的选项 74% 是答案，反过来说也有 26% 不是。听懂了就按听懂的选，没听懂再用技巧把三选一变成二选一。
- **数字是"整体上"的。** 某一次考试可能刚好反过来，出题人也知道这些说法。
- **同一条技巧，不同级别可能相反。** 最明显的是課題理解 的"最后定下来的才是答案"：N5 有 89% 的题如此，到 N2、N1 只剩三分之一。看你要考的那一列，不要拿别的级别的经验套。
- **"不灵"的技巧要主动丢掉。** 看到「必ず」就划掉，等于把一部分正解也划掉了：在 N1 真题里，带「必ず・全て・決して・常に」的选项有 37% 是正解，比瞎猜还高。
- **N5、N4 的考试次数少**（9 次、12 次），样本小的格子我们直接空着；给出的数字也比 N2、N1 的粗。
- 字面重合是用程序粗算的（选项里的 4 字片段有多少在试卷原文里出现过），只是一个大概。
:::
:::en
- **Tricks are for eliminating, not for replacing comprehension.** At N2 the apology is the answer 74% of the time — which also means 26% of the time it isn't. If you understood the exchange, go with what you understood; use the tricks to turn three options into two when you didn't.
- **The figures are averages.** One sitting can go the other way, and the people who write the test have heard these tricks too.
- **The same trick can flip between levels.** The clearest case is "whatever gets settled last is the answer" in task listening: true in 89% of N5 items, but only a third at N2 and N1. Read the column for your level; don't carry over what worked at another one.
- **Drop the tricks that don't work.** Crossing out every option with 必ず also crosses out real answers: on N1 papers, options with 必ず, 全て, 決して or 常に were the answer 37% of the time — more than a guess.
- **N5 and N4 have fewer sittings** (9 and 12), so cells with small samples are left empty, and the figures given are rougher than at N2 and N1.
- Word overlap was measured roughly by script (how many 4-character pieces of an option also appear elsewhere in the test booklet), so treat it as an estimate.
:::

## 排序题 ★：语法接续比句意更可靠||Sentence Order ★: Grammar Connections Beat Meaning

:::zh
做法分三步：

1. **先看空格后面接什么。** 最后一个空格要和句子剩下的部分接得上：后面是名词，最后一格就要是连体形或「の」；后面是「ように」「ほど」这类，前面要能接上它。
2. **再读懂句子大概在说什么**，像拼图一样先拼出两块明显连在一起的（「〜という＋名词」「〜によって＋变化」）。
3. **用接续把剩下的排掉。** 句意可能有两种都"说得通"的顺序，但接续通常只有一种是对的。所以在一定程度上可以认为：**语法接续 > 句意**。哪怕整句翻译不出来，接续不会骗人。

接续速查：

| 后面接什么 | 前面要用 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| 名词（连体） | 动词普通形（辞书形・ない形・た形・ている） | 読む本、読まない本、読んだ本 |
| 名词（连体） | い形容词原形 | 高い山 |
| 名词（连体） | な形容词＋な | 静かな公園 |
| 名词（连体） | 名词＋の | 学校の先生 |
| 动词・形容词（连用） | 动词て形 / ます形词干 | 書き続ける、行ってみる |
| 动词（连用） | い形容词 い→く | 高くなる |
| 动词（连用） | な形容词＋に | 静かに歩く |
| 中顿（并列） | い形容词 い→くて、な形容词＋で | 高くて広い、静かで広い |

本站的例子：

> この店のラーメンは、＿＿ ＿＿ ★ ＿＿ おいしい。 1 飽きない　2 毎日　3 ほど　4 食べても

- 句尾是「おいしい」，「ほど＋形容词」最自然 → 第 4 格是 ほど。
- 「ほど」前面要连体形 → 飽きない。
- 「〜ても」是连用接续，「毎日食べても飽きない」→ 毎日・食べても・飽きない・ほど。
- ★ 是第 3 格 → 答案 1（飽きない）。

N5、N4 的排序题多是两个人的对话，空格常在「〜を」「〜に」这样的助词前后：先找"名词＋助词"能配成一对的，再看哪个动词放在最后。

真题里 ★ 绝大多数在第 3 格（N5 89%、N4 100%、N3 92%、N2 83%、N1 91%），其余多在第 2 格。知道这一点可以帮你检查：排出来的顺序如果让 ★ 落在一个奇怪的位置，再看一遍题干上的空格。但答案号码本身很平均（见下面"猜哪个"），★ 位置帮不了你猜。
:::
:::en
Three steps:

1. **Look at what follows the blanks first.** The last blank has to join the rest of the sentence: if a noun follows, the last piece must be a noun-modifying form or end in の; if ように or ほど follows, the piece before it has to connect to that.
2. **Get the rough meaning, then join the two pieces that obviously belong together** (〜という + noun, 〜によって + change), like a jigsaw.
3. **Use connections to place the rest.** Two orders can both "make sense", but usually only one connects correctly. So to a point: **grammar connections beat meaning** — even when you can't translate the whole sentence, the connections don't lie.

Connection cheat sheet:

| What follows | Form needed before it | Example |
|------|------|------|
| A noun (modifying) | Plain verb form (dictionary, ない, た, ている) | 読む本, 読まない本, 読んだ本 |
| A noun (modifying) | い-adjective as is | 高い山 |
| A noun (modifying) | な-adjective + な | 静かな公園 |
| A noun (modifying) | Noun + の | 学校の先生 |
| A verb / adjective (linking) | Verb て-form / ます stem | 書き続ける, 行ってみる |
| A verb (linking) | い-adjective い→く | 高くなる |
| A verb (linking) | な-adjective + に | 静かに歩く |
| A pause / "and" | い→くて, な-adjective + で | 高くて広い, 静かで広い |

Our own example:

> この店のラーメンは、＿＿ ＿＿ ★ ＿＿ おいしい。 1 飽きない　2 毎日　3 ほど　4 食べても

- The sentence ends in おいしい, and ほど + adjective is the natural fit → slot 4 is ほど.
- ほど needs a noun-modifying form before it → 飽きない.
- 〜ても links to what follows: 毎日食べても飽きない → 毎日, 食べても, 飽きない, ほど.
- The ★ is slot 3 → answer 1 (飽きない).

At N5 and N4 most sentence-order items are two-person exchanges, and the blanks sit around particles like 〜を and 〜に: pair each noun with its particle first, then see which verb goes last.

On past papers the ★ sits in the 3rd slot nearly every time (N5 89%, N4 100%, N3 92%, N2 83%, N1 91%), otherwise mostly the 2nd. Use it as a check: if your order puts the ★ somewhere odd, look at the blanks in the question again. The answer numbers themselves are evenly spread (see "If You Have to Guess"), so the ★ slot won't help you guess.
:::

## 阅读||Reading

### 通用：先题后文，定位找答案||For Every Passage: Question First, Then Find the Spot

:::zh
- **先看问题，再读文章**，带着问题去定位段落，不必通篇读懂。
- **转折词后面是重点**：「しかし」「実は」「ところが」（N5、N4 是「でも」）后面通常是作者真正想说的。
- **主旨多在最后一段**，前面的经历、例子是用来引出观点的。
- **例子只是为了说明观点**，答案在例子前后的那句总结里，不要陷进例子的细节。
- **两个选项很像时，答案多半在这两个里**（N3 65%、N2 73%、N1 67%；N5、N4 也有 57–59%），先比较它们差在哪个词上。
- **N5、N4 的短文就是几行字**：问题多是"谁、什么时候、要带什么"，在文章里找到那一句就够了；中篇问"为什么""怎么想"，答案常在「〜から」「〜ので」那一句。
- **一篇 N2、N1 4–5 分钟，N3 以下 2–3 分钟**，卡住的题先标记，不要恋战。
- 回原文找**意思相同、换了说法**的选项。但上面的数据也说明：不要因为一个选项"和原文字句一样"就把它划掉，各级真题里这种选项并不比别的更常错；和原文字面最不像的才更常错（N5 只有 10% 是答案）。
:::
:::en
- **Read the question first, then the passage**, and go looking for the paragraph that answers it — you don't need to understand every line.
- **What comes after a contrast word matters most**: after しかし, 実は or ところが (でも at N5 and N4) is usually what the author really means.
- **The main point is usually in the last paragraph**; experiences and examples earlier on lead up to it.
- **Examples are there to illustrate a point**: the answer is the summary sentence before or after the example, not its details.
- **When two options look alike, the answer is usually one of them** (65% at N3, 73% at N2, 67% at N1; 57–59% at N5 and N4 too) — compare the word where they differ.
- **N5 and N4 short passages are a few lines**: the question is usually who, when or what to bring — find that one sentence. Mid-length passages ask why or what someone thinks; the answer is often in the 〜から / 〜ので sentence.
- **4–5 minutes per passage at N2 and N1, 2–3 below that**; mark a hard question and move on.
- Look for the option that says **the same thing in other words**. But as the data above shows, don't cross out an option just because it uses the passage's own words — at no level are those wrong more often than the rest; it's the option least like the passage that's wrong more often (only 10% were the answer at N5).
:::

### 按文章类型||By Kind of Passage

:::zh
| 类型 | 特点 | 怎么读 |
|------|------|------|
| 说明文・科普（讲原理、实验、自然现象） | 术语和专有名词多，但不影响做题；层次不深，题目基本按原文内容问 | 抓"因为…所以…"的因果链，专有名词直接跳过；读不懂的句子先放下，不要多看 |
| 随笔・叙事（个人经历、生活感悟） | 两层：故事经历（次要）＋作者感悟（核心）；"当年的我"和"现在回头看的我" | 别沉迷情节，题目几乎都问作者的领悟和想法；重点看后者，主旨基本在最后一段 |
| 评论・主张 | 作者对一个看法提出质疑或新观点，常有"我"、具体生活片段 | 找作者的立场句；"一般认为…但是…"后面才是作者的观点 |
| 综合理解（A・B 两篇，N2・N1） | 问两篇的共同点和不同点 | 忠于原文，不要自己发挥；先看问题，再分别在 A、B 里找对应的那一句 |
| 留言・邮件・通知（N5–N3 常见） | 谁写给谁、要对方做什么 | 先看最后几行：请求、日期、要带的东西多在那里 |
| 信息检索 | 表格、通知、广告 | 不用通读，按题目条件找：谁、什么、哪里、什么时候；注意"但是""除外"之类的附加条件 |
:::
:::en
| Kind | What it looks like | How to read it |
|------|------|------|
| Explanation / popular science (how something works, an experiment, nature) | Lots of terms and names, which don't get in the way; one layer deep, questions follow the content | Follow the because → so chain, skip proper names; leave a sentence you can't parse and keep going |
| Essay / personal story | Two layers: the story (secondary) and what the author learned (the point); "me back then" vs "me looking back now" | Don't get lost in the plot — questions almost always ask about the author's insight; it's usually in the last paragraph |
| Opinion / argument | The author questions a common view or proposes a new one, often with "I" and an everyday scene | Find the author's position; after "people often think… but…" comes the author's own view |
| Comparing two texts (A and B, N2 and N1) | Asks what the two agree and disagree on | Stay with the texts, don't add your own reasoning; read the question, then find the matching sentence in A and in B |
| Notes, emails, notices (common at N5–N3) | Who writes to whom, and what they want done | Read the last lines first: the request, the date and what to bring are usually there |
| Information retrieval | Tables, notices, adverts | No need to read it all — search by the question's conditions: who, what, where, when; watch for "but" and "except" conditions |
:::

## 听力||Listening

### 課題理解：N5・N4 听最后，N3 起盯住「まず」「〜てから」||Task-Based Items: At N5–N4 Listen to the End; From N3, Listen for まず and 〜てから

:::zh
这类题问"接下来（首先）要做什么""要带什么""在哪里集合"。我们把能拿到原文的真题逐题读了一遍（N5 5 次考试 35 题、N4 7 次 52 题、N3 29 次 174 题、N2 26 次 125 题、N1 27 次 149 题，由 AI 辅助逐题标注），结果各级差别很大：

| 说法 | N5 | N4 | N3 | N2 | N1 |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| 对话最后定下来的事就是答案 | 89% | 69% | 48% | 31% | 32% |
| 「まず・先に・最初に・〜てから・その前に」指向的就是答案 | — | 4/5 | 62% | 91% | 90% |
| 出现这类顺序词的题 | 6% | 10% | 21% | 约 1/4 | 约 1/4 |
| 最先被拿出来讨论的选项不是答案（随机 75%） | 69% | 95% | 85% | 81% | 82% |
| 答案紧跟在「でも・けど・ただ・あ、そうだ」之后 | 6% | 35% | 21% | 36% | 43% |
| 要行动的人自己提的方案被对方否掉 | 26% | 46% | 34% | 27% | 26% |
| 某个错误选项被聊得比答案还多 | — | — | — | 37% | 28% |

（最后一行 N5–N3 的标注口径和 N2、N1 不一样，不能直接比，所以不给。）

为什么会这样？N5、N4 的题多问"要带什么""几点去""在哪里"，对话就是一步步改主意，最后那个版本就是答案。到了 N3 一半的题、N2 和 N1 的大多数题问的是"**首先**做什么"：答案多在对话中段定下来（N2 53%、N1 51%），后面接着聊的是"做完之后再……"的事。所以：

- 先看清问题问的是"首先""接下来"还是"最后"。
- **N5、N4**：一直听到最后，改主意的地方（「あ、やっぱり」「じゃあ〜にしましょう」）最关键；数字、时间、东西一出现就记下来，划掉被改掉的。
- **N3 起**：听到「まず」「〜てから」「その前に」就在对应的选项旁边打记号：「AてからB」现在要做的是 A。
- 被修正、被否定、被说"那个之后再说"的选项划掉。
- 实在没听懂：答案在 2、3 的占 N4 61%、N3 61%、N2 66%、N1 64%（N5 54%，没有明显倾向）。
:::
:::en
These items ask "what will this person do (first / next)?", "what should they bring?", "where will they meet?". We went through every past-paper item whose script we have (N5: 5 sittings, 35 items; N4: 7, 52; N3: 29, 174; N2: 26, 125; N1: 27, 149; labelled item by item with AI help). The levels differ a lot:

| Tip | N5 | N4 | N3 | N2 | N1 |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| What gets decided at the very end is the answer | 89% | 69% | 48% | 31% | 32% |
| A sequencing word (まず, 先に, 最初に, 〜てから, その前に) points to the answer | — | 4/5 | 62% | 91% | 90% |
| Items that have such a sequencing word | 6% | 10% | 21% | about 1/4 | about 1/4 |
| The first option talked about is not the answer (chance 75%) | 69% | 95% | 85% | 81% | 82% |
| The answer comes right after でも, けど, ただ, あ、そうだ | 6% | 35% | 21% | 36% | 43% |
| The person who must act suggests something and is turned down | 26% | 46% | 34% | 27% | 26% |
| A wrong option gets more talk than the answer | — | — | — | 37% | 28% |

(The last row was labelled differently at N5–N3 and can't be compared, so it's left out.)

Why? N5 and N4 items mostly ask what to bring, what time or where, and the conversation changes its mind step by step — the final version is the answer. From half the N3 items to most N2 and N1 items, the question asks what to do **first**: the answer is mostly settled in the middle of the conversation (N2 53%, N1 51%), and the talk that follows is about what comes after. So:

- Check whether the question asks "first", "next" or "in the end".
- **N5 and N4**: listen to the very end; the change of mind (あ、やっぱり, じゃあ〜にしましょう) is the key moment. Write down numbers, times and things as you hear them, and cross out what gets changed.
- **From N3**: when you hear まず, 〜てから or その前に, mark the matching option: in "A てから B", what you do now is A.
- Cross out anything that gets corrected, rejected or put off with "let's do that later".
- If you're lost: the answer was 2 or 3 in 61% of N4 and N3 items, 66% at N2 and 64% at N1 (54% at N5 — no real lean).
:::

### ポイント理解：先知道问题，专听理由||Point Items: Know the Question, Listen for the Reason

:::zh
- 播放前有时间看选项，先把四个选项的区别看清楚，听的时候只等那个区别。
- 和課題理解 一样：转折词后面大概率是答案；被对方否定、修正的说法不选。
- 实在没听懂：答案在 2、3 的占 N4 58%、N3 57%、N2 66%、N1 57%；N5 只有 48%，等于没有倾向。
:::
:::en
- You get time to read the options before the audio: work out how the four differ, then listen only for that difference.
- As with task items: the answer usually follows a contrast word, and anything the other speaker denies or corrects is out.
- If you're lost: the answer was 2 or 3 in 58% of N4 items, 57% at N3, 66% at N2 and 57% at N1; at N5 only 48% — no lean at all.
:::

### 概要理解（N3–N1）：偏难，听开头和结尾||Summary Items (N3–N1): Hard — Listen to the Start and the End

:::zh
选项不印在题本上，问题也只在最后才说。最有效的办法是抓住开头那一段（说话人要谈什么）和最后的总结；中间的例子和细节不用记全。N2、N1 这个题型的答案位置很平均；N3 的答案很少是 1（90 题里只有 14%），其余三个差不多。N5、N4 没有这个题型。
:::
:::en
Nothing is printed and the question only comes at the end. What works best is catching the opening (what the speaker is going to talk about) and the closing summary; you don't need every example in between. At N2 and N1 the answer positions are evenly spread; at N3 the answer is rarely 1 (14% of 90 items) and the other three are about even. N5 and N4 don't have this item type.
:::

### 発話表現（N5–N3）：先想清楚"谁做、做什么"||Utterance Expressions (N5–N3): Work Out Who Does What

:::zh
看图听情景，选这个人该说的话，3 个选项。我们把常见的说法都拿 N5–N3 真题核对了（156 题）：「〜てくれる？・〜てもらえますか」这类请求的选项 32% 是答案，「〜ましょうか」这类提议 25%，反问 33%，重复情景里的词 30%——**没有哪一种句型本身比瞎猜（33%）明显好**。答案位置也平均（N5 34·29·37、N4 35·42·24、N3 38·32·30）。所以这一题只能靠听懂，做法是：

- 听情景时先确定**谁来做**：自己做（→「〜ましょうか」「〜てもいいですか」），还是请对方做（→「〜てくれませんか」「〜てもらえますか」）。
- 再确定**是什么场合**：请求、提议、请求许可、道歉、道谢、邀请。三个选项通常有两个"人称弄反了"（比如该请对方做却说成"我来做吧"）。
- 敬语看对象：对老师、客人、上司要用「〜ていただけませんか」「お〜します」。

这几条是做法，不是统计规律：真题里能检验的部分只说明"别靠句型猜"。
:::
:::en
You see a picture, hear the situation and pick what the person should say, from 3 options. We checked the usual cues against the N5–N3 papers (156 items): request forms like 〜てくれる？ / 〜てもらえますか were the answer 32% of the time, offers like 〜ましょうか 25%, questions back 33%, options repeating the situation's words 30% — **no sentence pattern beats a blind guess (33%) by itself**. Answer positions are even too (N5 34·29·37, N4 35·42·24, N3 38·32·30). So this item rewards understanding only. How to go about it:

- While the situation is described, settle **who acts**: you (→ 〜ましょうか, 〜てもいいですか) or the other person (→ 〜てくれませんか, 〜てもらえますか).
- Then settle **what kind of moment** it is: a request, an offer, asking permission, an apology, thanks, an invitation. Usually two of the three options have the roles the wrong way round (offering to do it yourself when you should ask the other person, say).
- Keigo follows the listener: to a teacher, customer or boss, 〜ていただけませんか and お〜します.

These are methods, not statistics: the part the papers can test only says "don't guess by sentence pattern".
:::

### 即時応答：用排除法把三选一变成二选一||Quick Response: Turn Three Options into Two

:::zh
先听懂题干的**语气**：陈述还是提问、抱怨还是道谢、是不是在请求。语气对了，答案的方向就对了：

- 抱怨、责备 → 回应多半是道歉（道歉的选项 N3 45%、N2 74%、N1 48% 是答案）；
- 对方在夸你 → 谦虚或道谢；
- 句尾上扬是在提问，要给出回答；对方说话吞吞吐吐、欲言又止，多半是有难处或不愿意。

然后排除：

- **重复题干原词的选项先怀疑**，这一条各级都灵（N5 20%、N4 8%、N3 14%、N2 22%、N1 17%）。敬语、固定搭配里的重复不算。N5、N4 的题少，能检验的就这一条。
- **授受表达（〜てあげる・〜てもらう）的选项**只在 N2 少是答案（16%），N3（35%）、N1（25%）不用管。
- **两长一短时，短的那个值得多看一眼**——只限 N2（43%）、N1（45%）；N3 不灵（29%）。
- **附和型的选项（「そうですね」「ええ」「うん」开头）值得优先考虑**（N3 50%、N2 57%、N1 42%）；N2、N1 以「じゃあ」「それなら」顺着往下接的选项多是错的（19% / 24%），N3 不太明显（28%）。
- 带否定（〜ない・〜ません）的选项并不"危险"：N2、N1 有 41–43% 是答案，N3 28%、N4 22%，和瞎猜差不多。
- "反问的不选""提议的要选"在真题里几乎没用，不要靠它们。
:::
:::en
First catch the **tone** of the prompt: a statement or a question, a complaint or thanks, a request? Get the tone right and the answer points the right way:

- A complaint or reproach → the reply is usually an apology (the apologising option was the answer 45% of the time at N3, 74% at N2, 48% at N1);
- Someone praises you → modesty or thanks;
- A rising tone at the end is a question that needs an answer; hesitant, trailing-off speech usually means a problem or a polite no.

Then eliminate:

- **Be suspicious of an option that repeats words from the prompt** — this works at every level (N5 20%, N4 8%, N3 14%, N2 22%, N1 17%). Repeats inside set phrases and keigo don't count. N5 and N4 have few items, and this is the only tip we could test there.
- **Giving/receiving forms (〜てあげる, 〜てもらう)** are rarely the answer only at N2 (16%); ignore this at N3 (35%) and N1 (25%).
- **With two long options and one short one, take a second look at the short one** — at N2 (43%) and N1 (45%) only; not at N3 (29%).
- **Agreeing replies (opening with そうですね, ええ or うん) deserve first look** (50% at N3, 57% at N2, 42% at N1); at N2 and N1, replies that run on with じゃあ or それなら are usually wrong (19% / 24%), less clearly at N3 (28%).
- Negative options (〜ない, 〜ません) aren't "risky": 41–43% of them were the answer at N2 and N1, 28% at N3 and 22% at N4 — about a guess.
- "Never pick the question back" and "pick the suggestion" barely work on real papers — don't lean on them.
:::

### 統合理解（N2・N1）：边听边记，讨论最多的往往是答案||Integrated Listening (N2 and N1): Take Notes; What Gets Discussed Most Often Wins

:::zh
- 第 1、2 题通常是三个人讨论（比如上司和两个下属）：一个人提出方案，另外两人提出顾虑或新的提议，最后由一个人定下来。记下每个方案被谁否定了、最后谁拍板。
- 听到「まず」「一番」「重要なのは」后面的内容，往往就是答案；「〜てから」「〜次第」后面的是之后才做的事，不是现在选的。
- 题与题之间间隔比較长，利用这段时间整理笔记里的关键词。
- 实在没听懂：真题里答案在 2、3 的占 N2 58%、N1 56%。
:::
:::en
- The first items are usually a three-way discussion (say, a manager and two staff): one person proposes something, the others raise concerns or new ideas, and one of them decides. Note who rejected which plan and who made the call.
- What follows まず, 一番 or 重要なのは is often the answer; what follows 〜てから or 〜次第 is what happens later, not what's chosen now.
- The gaps between items are longer here — use them to tidy the keywords in your notes.
- If you're lost: the answer was 2 or 3 in 58% of N2 items and 56% of N1 items.
:::

## 实在不会：猜哪个||If You Have to Guess

:::zh
空着一定没分，猜至少有机会。真题里各题型的正确答案是 1、2、3、4 的比例（%）：

| 题型 | N5 | N4 | N3 | N2 | N1 | 怎么猜 |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| 听力 課題理解 | 20·27·27·27 | 20·29·32·19 | 20·30·31·20 | 16·34·32·18 | 21·35·29·16 | N4 起猜 2 或 3 |
| 听力 ポイント理解 | 21·24·24·31 | 22·30·28·20 | 18·29·28·26 | 17·32·34·18 | 22·28·29·21 | N4 起稍偏 2、3 |
| 听力 統合理解 | — | — | — | 22·28·30·20 | 22·26·30·23 | 稍偏 2、3 |
| 听力 概要理解 | — | — | 14·30·26·30 | 28·20·24·28 | 23·24·25·29 | N3 别猜 1，其余没有规律 |
| 听力 発話表現（3 选 1） | 34·29·37 | 35·42·24 | 38·32·30 | — | — | 没有规律，靠听懂 |
| 听力 即時応答（3 选 1） | 28·33·39 | 28·35·37 | 34·36·31 | 32·36·32 | 34·34·32 | 没有规律，用排除法 |
| 阅读（中篇） | 6·28·39·28 | 21·26·17·36 | 18·24·28·30 | 27·22·25·26 | 25·25·28·23 | 题太少（N5 18 题、N4 42 题），没有可靠规律 |
| 排序题 | 28·28·25·20 | 23·21·34·23 | 25·27·24·23 | 24·26·26·25 | 29·24·21·26 | 没有规律 |

阅读和文字语法部分的答案位置基本平均，不要指望靠位置猜。N5 听力题量少（每个题型 35–49 题），表里的差别大多是偶然。

还有一条各级都一样：**同一大题里，相邻两题答案相同的只有 14–16%**（随机的话是 25–33%），连续三题相同只有 1–2%。所以猜的时候别选和上一题一样的号；如果你连着三题都选了同一个号，回头检查一下。
:::
:::en
A blank scores nothing; a guess at least has a chance. Share of past-paper answers that were option 1, 2, 3 or 4 (%):

| Item type | N5 | N4 | N3 | N2 | N1 | How to guess |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| Listening: task | 20·27·27·27 | 20·29·32·19 | 20·30·31·20 | 16·34·32·18 | 21·35·29·16 | From N4, guess 2 or 3 |
| Listening: point | 21·24·24·31 | 22·30·28·20 | 18·29·28·26 | 17·32·34·18 | 22·28·29·21 | From N4, lean to 2 or 3 |
| Listening: integrated | — | — | — | 22·28·30·20 | 22·26·30·23 | Lean to 2 or 3 |
| Listening: summary | — | — | 14·30·26·30 | 28·20·24·28 | 23·24·25·29 | At N3 avoid 1; otherwise no pattern |
| Listening: utterance expressions (3 options) | 34·29·37 | 35·42·24 | 38·32·30 | — | — | No pattern — understand it |
| Listening: quick response (3 options) | 28·33·39 | 28·35·37 | 34·36·31 | 32·36·32 | 34·34·32 | No pattern — eliminate |
| Reading (mid-length) | 6·28·39·28 | 21·26·17·36 | 18·24·28·30 | 27·22·25·26 | 25·25·28·23 | Too few items (N5 18, N4 42) — no reliable pattern |
| Sentence order | 28·28·25·20 | 23·21·34·23 | 25·27·24·23 | 24·26·26·25 | 29·24·21·26 | No pattern |

Reading, vocabulary and grammar answers are spread evenly — position won't help you there. N5 listening has few items (35–49 per type), so most of its differences are chance.

One more, the same at every level: **within a section, two neighbouring items share the same answer only 14–16% of the time** (25–33% by chance), and three in a row only 1–2%. So when guessing, avoid the number you gave the previous item — and if you've picked the same number three times running, check them again.
:::
