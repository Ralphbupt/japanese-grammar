# N2・N1 考前技巧冲刺：哪些做题技巧在真题里真的灵||JLPT N2 & N1 Last-Minute Tips — Which Tricks Actually Work on Past Papers

:::zh
考前最后几周，到处都是"做题技巧"：反问句的选项不选、道歉的要选、带「必ず・全て」的选项大多是错的、照搬原文的选项不选……这些说法有的很灵，有的在真题里根本不成立。这一页把 N2、N1 最常听到的技巧整理在一起，**每一条都拿 2010–2026 年的真题（N2、N1 各 32 次考试）核对过**：带这个特征的选项，真正是正解的比例是多少，和瞎猜相比是好是坏。页面里只给统计数字，不引用真题原文；示例句都是本站新写的。

用法：先看下面的总表，知道哪些技巧可以放心用、哪些要丢掉；再按部分（排序、阅读、听力）看具体做法；最后是"实在不会时猜哪个"。
:::
:::en
In the last weeks before the test, "exam tricks" are everywhere: never pick the answer that asks a question back, always pick the apology, options with 必ず or 全て are usually wrong, options that copy the passage word for word are traps… Some of these work well; some simply don't hold on real papers. This page collects the tips you hear most for N2 and N1 and **checks each one against the 2010–2026 past papers (32 sittings each for N2 and N1)**: when an option has the feature, how often is it really the answer — better or worse than a blind guess? Only the counts are shown, no past-paper text; every example sentence here is our own.

How to use it: start with the summary table to see which tricks to keep and which to drop, then read the technique for each part (sentence order, reading, listening), and finally what to pick when you have to guess.
:::

## 怎么读这些数字||How to Read the Numbers

:::zh
- **技巧是用来排除的，不是用来代替理解的。** 道歉的选项 74% 是答案，反过来说也有 26% 不是。听懂了就按听懂的选，没听懂再用技巧把三选一变成二选一。
- **数字是"整体上"的。** 某一次考试可能刚好反过来，出题人也知道这些说法。
- **"不灵"的技巧要主动丢掉。** 看到「必ず」就划掉，等于把一部分正解也划掉了：在 N1 真题里，带「必ず・全て・決して・常に」的选项有 37% 是正解，比瞎猜还高。
- 字面重合是用程序粗算的（选项里的 4 字片段有多少在试卷原文里出现过），只是一个大概。
:::
:::en
- **Tricks are for eliminating, not for replacing comprehension.** The apology is the answer 74% of the time at N2 — which also means 26% of the time it isn't. If you understood the exchange, go with what you understood; use the tricks to turn three options into two when you didn't.
- **The figures are averages.** One sitting can go the other way, and the people who write the test have heard these tricks too.
- **Drop the tricks that don't work.** Crossing out every option with 必ず also crosses out real answers: on N1 papers, options with 必ず, 全て, 決して or 常に were the answer 37% of the time — more than a guess.
- Word overlap was measured roughly by script (how many 4-character pieces of an option also appear in the test booklet), so treat it as an estimate.
:::

## 排序题 ★：语法接续比句意更可靠||Sentence Order ★: Grammar Connections Beat Meaning

:::zh
做法分三步：

1. **先看空格后面接什么。** 最后一个空格要和句子剩下的部分接得上：后面是名词，最后一格就要是连体形或「の」；后面是「ように」「ほど」这类，前面要能接上它。
2. **再读懂句子大概在说什么**，像拼图一样先拼出两块明显连在一起的（「〜という＋名词」「〜によって＋变化」）。
3. **用接续把剩下的排掉。** 句意可能有两种都"说得通"的顺序，但接续通常只有一种是对的。所以在一定程度上可以认为：**语法接续 > 句意**。哪怕整句翻译不出来，接续不会骗人。

接续速查：

| 后面接什么 | 前面要用 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| 名词（连体） | 动词普通形（辞书形・ない形・た形・ている） | 読む本、読まない本、読んだ本 |
| 名词（连体） | い形容词原形 | 高い山 |
| 名词（连体） | な形容词＋な | 静かな公園 |
| 名词（连体） | 名词＋の | 学校の先生 |
| 动词・形容词（连用） | 动词て形 / ます形词干 | 書き続ける、行ってみる |
| 动词（连用） | い形容词 い→く | 高くなる |
| 动词（连用） | な形容词＋に | 静かに歩く |
| 中顿（并列） | い形容词 い→くて、な形容词＋で | 高くて広い、静かで広い |

本站的例子：

> この店のラーメンは、＿＿ ＿＿ ★ ＿＿ おいしい。 1 飽きない　2 毎日　3 ほど　4 食べても

- 句尾是「おいしい」，「ほど＋形容词」最自然 → 第 4 格是 ほど。
- 「ほど」前面要连体形 → 飽きない。
- 「〜ても」是连用接续，「毎日食べても飽きない」→ 毎日・食べても・飽きない・ほど。
- ★ 是第 3 格 → 答案 1（飽きない）。

真题里 ★ 多在第 3 格（N2 63%、N1 75%），其次第 2 格，几乎不会在第 1、4 格。知道这一点可以帮你检查：排出来的顺序如果让 ★ 落在一个奇怪的位置，再看一遍题干上的空格。
:::
:::en
Three steps:

1. **Look at what follows the blanks first.** The last blank has to join the rest of the sentence: if a noun follows, the last piece must be a noun-modifying form or end in の; if ように or ほど follows, the piece before it has to connect to that.
2. **Get the rough meaning, then join the two pieces that obviously belong together** (〜という + noun, 〜によって + change), like a jigsaw.
3. **Use connections to place the rest.** Two orders can both "make sense", but usually only one connects correctly. So to a point: **grammar connections beat meaning** — even when you can't translate the whole sentence, the connections don't lie.

Connection cheat sheet:

| What follows | Form needed before it | Example |
|------|------|------|
| A noun (modifying) | Plain verb form (dictionary, ない, た, ている) | 読む本, 読まない本, 読んだ本 |
| A noun (modifying) | い-adjective as is | 高い山 |
| A noun (modifying) | な-adjective + な | 静かな公園 |
| A noun (modifying) | Noun + の | 学校の先生 |
| A verb / adjective (linking) | Verb て-form / ます stem | 書き続ける, 行ってみる |
| A verb (linking) | い-adjective い→く | 高くなる |
| A verb (linking) | な-adjective + に | 静かに歩く |
| A pause / "and" | い→くて, な-adjective + で | 高くて広い, 静かで広い |

Our own example:

> この店のラーメンは、＿＿ ＿＿ ★ ＿＿ おいしい。 1 飽きない　2 毎日　3 ほど　4 食べても

- The sentence ends in おいしい, and ほど + adjective is the natural fit → slot 4 is ほど.
- ほど needs a noun-modifying form before it → 飽きない.
- 〜ても links to what follows: 毎日食べても飽きない → 毎日, 食べても, 飽きない, ほど.
- The ★ is slot 3 → answer 1 (飽きない).

On past papers the ★ sits in the 3rd slot most of the time (N2 63%, N1 75%), otherwise the 2nd — almost never the 1st or 4th. Use it as a check: if your order puts the ★ somewhere odd, look at the blanks in the question again.
:::

## 阅读||Reading

### 通用：先题后文，定位找答案||For Every Passage: Question First, Then Find the Spot

:::zh
- **先看问题，再读文章**，带着问题去定位段落，不必通篇读懂。
- **转折词后面是重点**：「しかし」「実は」「ところが」后面通常是作者真正想说的。
- **主旨多在最后一段**，前面的经历、例子是用来引出观点的。
- **例子只是为了说明观点**，答案在例子前后的那句总结里，不要陷进例子的细节。
- **一篇 4–5 分钟**，卡住的题先标记，不要恋战。
- 回原文找**意思相同、换了说法**的选项。但上面的数据也说明：不要因为一个选项"和原文字句一样"就把它划掉，真题里这种选项反而更常是正解，和原文最不像的才更常错。
:::
:::en
- **Read the question first, then the passage**, and go looking for the paragraph that answers it — you don't need to understand every line.
- **What comes after a contrast word matters most**: after しかし, 実は or ところが is usually what the author really means.
- **The main point is usually in the last paragraph**; experiences and examples earlier on lead up to it.
- **Examples are there to illustrate a point**: the answer is the summary sentence before or after the example, not its details.
- **4–5 minutes per passage**; mark a hard question and move on.
- Look for the option that says **the same thing in other words**. But as the data above shows, don't cross out an option just because it uses the passage's own words — on real papers those are the answer more often, and the option least like the passage is wrong more often.
:::

### 按文章类型||By Kind of Passage

:::zh
| 类型 | 特点 | 怎么读 |
|------|------|------|
| 说明文・科普（讲原理、实验、自然现象） | 术语和专有名词多，但不影响做题；层次不深，题目基本按原文内容问 | 抓"因为…所以…"的因果链，专有名词直接跳过；读不懂的句子先放下，不要多看 |
| 随笔・叙事（个人经历、生活感悟） | 两层：故事经历（次要）＋作者感悟（核心）；"当年的我"和"现在回头看的我" | 别沉迷情节，题目几乎都问作者的领悟和想法；重点看后者，主旨基本在最后一段 |
| 评论・主张 | 作者对一个看法提出质疑或新观点，常有"我"、具体生活片段 | 找作者的立场句；"一般认为…但是…"后面才是作者的观点 |
| 综合理解（A・B 两篇） | 问两篇的共同点和不同点 | 忠于原文，不要自己发挥；先看问题，再分别在 A、B 里找对应的那一句 |
| 信息检索 | 表格、通知、广告 | 不用通读，按题目条件找：谁、什么、哪里、什么时候；注意"但是""除外"之类的附加条件 |
:::
:::en
| Kind | What it looks like | How to read it |
|------|------|------|
| Explanation / popular science (how something works, an experiment, nature) | Lots of terms and names, which don't get in the way; one layer deep, questions follow the content | Follow the because → so chain, skip proper names; leave a sentence you can't parse and keep going |
| Essay / personal story | Two layers: the story (secondary) and what the author learned (the point); "me back then" vs "me looking back now" | Don't get lost in the plot — questions almost always ask about the author's insight; it's usually in the last paragraph |
| Opinion / argument | The author questions a common view or proposes a new one, often with "I" and an everyday scene | Find the author's position; after "people often think… but…" comes the author's own view |
| Comparing two texts (A and B) | Asks what the two agree and disagree on | Stay with the texts, don't add your own reasoning; read the question, then find the matching sentence in A and in B |
| Information retrieval | Tables, notices, adverts | No need to read it all — search by the question's conditions: who, what, where, when; watch for "but" and "except" conditions |
:::

## 听力||Listening

### 課題理解：盯住「まず」「〜てから」，不是"最后的决定"||Task-Based Items: Listen for まず and 〜てから, Not "the Last Decision"

:::zh
这类题问"接下来（首先）要做什么"，题本上印着四个选项。我们把能拿到原文的真题逐题读了一遍（N2 26 次考试 125 题、N1 27 次 149 题，由 AI 辅助逐题标注），结果：

| 说法 | 真题 N2 / N1 | 结论 |
|------|------|------|
| 「まず・先に・最初に・〜てから・その前に」指向的就是答案 | 91% / 90% | ✔ 最靠谱，约四分之一的题会出现 |
| 对话最后定下来的事就是答案 | 31% / 32% 的题如此 | ✘ 多数题不是 |
| 最先被拿出来讨论的选项不是答案 | 81% / 82% | △ 只比瞎猜（75%）略好 |
| 答案紧跟在「でも・けど・ただ・あ、そうだ」之后 | 36% / 43% 的题如此 | 常见，但不是每题都有 |
| 要行动的人自己提的方案被对方否掉 | 27% / 26% 的题如此 | 出现时，被否掉的不选 |
| 某个错误选项被聊得比答案还多 | 37% / 28% 的题如此 | 聊得多不等于答案 |

为什么"最后的决定"不灵？因为问的往往是"**首先**做什么"：答案多在对话中段定下来（N2 53%、N1 51%），后面接着聊的是"做完之后再……"的事。所以：

- 先看清问题问的是"首先""接下来"还是"最后"。
- 听到「まず」「〜てから」「その前に」就在对应的选项旁边打记号：「AてからB」现在要做的是 A。
- 被修正、被否定、被说"那个之后再说"的选项划掉。
- 实在没听懂：答案在 2、3 的占 N2 66%、N1 64%。
:::
:::en
These items ask "what will this person do (first / next)?" with four printed options. We went through every past-paper item whose script we have (N2: 26 sittings, 125 items; N1: 27 sittings, 149 items; labelled item by item with AI help). The results:

| Tip | Past papers N2 / N1 | Verdict |
|------|------|------|
| A sequencing word (まず, 先に, 最初に, 〜てから, その前に) points to the answer | 91% / 90% | ✔ The most reliable; in about a quarter of items |
| What gets decided at the very end is the answer | true in 31% / 32% | ✘ Not in most items |
| The first option talked about is not the answer | 81% / 82% | △ A little better than chance (75%) |
| The answer comes right after でも, けど, ただ, あ、そうだ | true in 36% / 43% | Common, not always |
| The person who must act suggests something and is turned down | true in 27% / 26% | Then drop that option |
| A wrong option gets more talk than the answer | true in 37% / 28% | Most talked about ≠ answer |

Why doesn't "the last decision wins" hold? Because the question usually asks what to do **first**: the answer is mostly settled in the middle of the conversation (N2 53%, N1 51%), and the talk that follows is about what comes after. So:

- Check whether the question asks "first", "next" or "in the end".
- When you hear まず, 〜てから or その前に, mark the matching option: in "A てから B", what you do now is A.
- Cross out anything that gets corrected, rejected or put off with "let's do that later".
- If you're lost: the answer was 2 or 3 in 66% of N2 items and 64% of N1 items.
:::

### ポイント理解：先知道问题，专听理由||Point Items: Know the Question, Listen for the Reason

:::zh
- 播放前有时间看选项，先把四个选项的区别看清楚，听的时候只等那个区别。
- 和課題理解 一样：转折词后面大概率是答案；被对方否定、修正的说法不选。
- 实在没听懂：答案在 2、3 的占 N2 66%、N1 57%。
:::
:::en
- You get time to read the options before the audio: work out how the four differ, then listen only for that difference.
- As with task items: the answer usually follows a contrast word, and anything the other speaker denies or corrects is out.
- If you're lost: the answer was 2 or 3 in 66% of N2 items and 57% of N1 items.
:::

### 概要理解：偏难，听开头和结尾||Summary Items: Hard — Listen to the Start and the End

:::zh
选项不印在题本上，问题也只在最后才说。最有效的办法是抓住开头那一段（说话人要谈什么）和最后的总结；中间的例子和细节不用记全。这个题型的答案位置在真题里很平均，没有可猜的规律。
:::
:::en
Nothing is printed and the question only comes at the end. What works best is catching the opening (what the speaker is going to talk about) and the closing summary; you don't need every example in between. Answer positions on past papers are evenly spread here — there's no guessing pattern.
:::

### 即時応答：用排除法把三选一变成二选一||Quick Response: Turn Three Options into Two

:::zh
先听懂题干的**语气**：陈述还是提问、抱怨还是道谢、是不是在请求。语气对了，答案的方向就对了：

- 抱怨、责备 → 回应多半是道歉（真题里道歉的选项 N2 74% 是答案）；
- 对方在夸你 → 谦虚或道谢；
- 句尾上扬是在提问，要给出回答；对方说话吞吞吐吐、欲言又止，多半是有难处或不愿意。

然后排除：

- **重复题干原词的选项先怀疑**（N2 只有 22%、N1 17% 是答案）。敬语、固定搭配里的重复不算。
- **授受表达（〜てあげる・〜てもらう）的选项先怀疑**，N2 尤其（16%）。
- **两长一短时，短的那个值得多看一眼**（N2 43%、N1 45%）。
- "反问的不选""提议的要选"在真题里几乎没用，不要靠它们。
:::
:::en
First catch the **tone** of the prompt: a statement or a question, a complaint or thanks, a request? Get the tone right and the answer points the right way:

- A complaint or reproach → the reply is usually an apology (at N2 the apologising option was the answer 74% of the time);
- Someone praises you → modesty or thanks;
- A rising tone at the end is a question that needs an answer; hesitant, trailing-off speech usually means a problem or a polite no.

Then eliminate:

- **Be suspicious of an option that repeats words from the prompt** (only 22% at N2 and 17% at N1 were the answer). Repeats inside set phrases and keigo don't count.
- **Be suspicious of giving/receiving forms (〜てあげる, 〜てもらう)**, especially at N2 (16%).
- **With two long options and one short one, take a second look at the short one** (43% at N2, 45% at N1).
- "Never pick the question back" and "pick the suggestion" barely work on real papers — don't lean on them.
:::

### 統合理解：边听边记，讨论最多的往往是答案||Integrated Listening: Take Notes; What Gets Discussed Most Often Wins

:::zh
- 第 1、2 题通常是三个人讨论（比如上司和两个下属）：一个人提出方案，另外两人提出顾虑或新的提议，最后由一个人定下来。记下每个方案被谁否定了、最后谁拍板。
- 听到「まず」「一番」「重要なのは」后面的内容，往往就是答案；「〜てから」「〜次第」后面的是之后才做的事，不是现在选的。
- 题与题之间间隔比較长，利用这段时间整理笔记里的关键词。
- 实在没听懂：真题里答案在 2、3 的占 N2 58%、N1 56%。
:::
:::en
- The first items are usually a three-way discussion (say, a manager and two staff): one person proposes something, the others raise concerns or new ideas, and one of them decides. Note who rejected which plan and who made the call.
- What follows まず, 一番 or 重要なのは is often the answer; what follows 〜てから or 〜次第 is what happens later, not what's chosen now.
- The gaps between items are longer here — use them to tidy the keywords in your notes.
- If you're lost: the answer was 2 or 3 in 58% of N2 items and 56% of N1 items.
:::

## 实在不会：猜哪个||If You Have to Guess

:::zh

| 题型 | N2：选 1·2·3·4（%） | N1：选 1·2·3·4（%） | 怎么猜 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 听力 課題理解 | 16·34·32·18 | 21·35·29·16 | 猜 2 或 3 |
| 听力 ポイント理解 | 17·32·34·18 | 22·28·29·21 | 猜 2 或 3 |
| 听力 統合理解 | 22·28·30·20 | 22·26·30·23 | 稍偏 2、3 |
| 听力 概要理解 | 28·20·24·28 | 23·24·25·29 | 没有规律 |
| 听力 即時応答（3 选 1） | 32·36·32 | 34·34·32 | 没有规律，用排除法 |
| 阅读（中篇） | 27·22·25·26 | 25·25·28·23 | 没有规律 |

阅读和文字语法部分的答案位置基本平均，不要指望靠位置猜。
:::
:::en
A blank scores nothing; a guess at least has a chance. Share of past-paper answers that were option 1, 2, 3 or 4:

| Listening: summary | 28·20·24·28 | 23·24·25·29 | No pattern |

Reading, vocabulary and grammar answers are spread evenly — position won't help you there.
