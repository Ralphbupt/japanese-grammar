# JLPT 日本语能力考试是什么：级别、题型、分数和报名||What Is the JLPT? Levels, Test Format, Scores & Registration

:::zh
**JLPT（日本語能力試験，日本语能力考试）**是给母语不是日语的人考的日语水平考试，由日本国际交流基金会和日本国际教育支援协会（JEES）主办，日本国内和全世界很多城市都能考。它分 **N5 到 N1 五个级别**，N5 最简单，N1 最难；**全部是选择题**，只考读和听，不考口语和写作。每年考两次，一般在 **7 月和 12 月的第一个星期日**。这一页把级别、题型、分数线、报名和出成绩的时间放在一起，最后列出每个级别在本站能免费练的内容。
:::
:::en
**The JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test, 日本語能力試験)** is the standard Japanese test for people whose first language isn't Japanese. It's run by the Japan Foundation and Japan Educational Exchanges and Services (JEES) and held in Japan and in cities around the world. There are **five levels, N5 to N1**: N5 is the easiest, N1 the hardest. **Every question is multiple choice** — it tests reading and listening only, with no speaking or writing. It's held twice a year, usually on the **first Sunday of July and of December**. This page puts the levels, the test format, the pass marks, registration and results in one place, then lists what you can practise for free here at each level.
:::

## 考什么：题型和时长||What's on the Test: Sections and Timing

:::zh
每一级都考三部分：**言语知识**（文字・词汇・语法）、**阅读**、**听力**。N1、N2 的言语知识和阅读在同一场考；N3 到 N5 分成两场。

| 级别 | 言语知识・阅读 | 听力 |
|------|------|------|
| N1 | 110 分钟（同一场） | 55 分钟 |
| N2 | 105 分钟（同一场） | 50 分钟 |
| N3 | 30 分钟（文字・词汇）＋ 70 分钟（语法・阅读） | 40 分钟 |
| N4 | 25 分钟（文字・词汇）＋ 55 分钟（语法・阅读） | 35 分钟 |
| N5 | 20 分钟（文字・词汇）＋ 40 分钟（语法・阅读） | 30 分钟 |

每种题型（汉字读音、选词填空、句子排序、听力的即时应答……）怎么做，见[文字词汇与语法做题技巧](/jlpt-vocabulary-grammar-tips/)、[阅读技巧](/jlpt-reading-tips/)和[听力技巧](/jlpt-listening-tips/)。
:::
:::en
Every level has three parts: **language knowledge** (vocabulary and grammar), **reading** and **listening**. At N1 and N2, language knowledge and reading are one sitting; at N3–N5 they're split into two.

| Level | Language knowledge & reading | Listening |
|------|------|------|
| N1 | 110 min (one sitting) | 55 min |
| N2 | 105 min (one sitting) | 50 min |
| N3 | 30 min (vocabulary) + 70 min (grammar & reading) | 40 min |
| N4 | 25 min (vocabulary) + 55 min (grammar & reading) | 35 min |
| N5 | 20 min (vocabulary) + 40 min (grammar & reading) | 30 min |

How to approach each item type (kanji reading, word in context, sentence order, quick response in listening …): see the [vocabulary & grammar tips](/jlpt-vocabulary-grammar-tips/), [reading tips](/jlpt-reading-tips/) and [listening tips](/jlpt-listening-tips/).
:::

## 分数和合格线||Scores and Pass Marks

:::zh
满分都是 180 分。要合格，**总分要过线，每一科也要过单科最低分**——总分再高，有一科没到最低分也不合格。

| 级别 | 合格线 | 各科满分 | 单科最低分 |
|------|------|------|------|
| N1 | 100 / 180 | 言语知识 60・阅读 60・听力 60 | 每科 19 |
| N2 | 90 / 180 | 言语知识 60・阅读 60・听力 60 | 每科 19 |
| N3 | 95 / 180 | 言语知识 60・阅读 60・听力 60 | 每科 19 |
| N4 | 90 / 180 | 言语知识・阅读 120・听力 60 | 38 和 19 |
| N5 | 80 / 180 | 言语知识・阅读 120・听力 60 | 38 和 19 |

分数是按题目难度换算的"标准分"，不是简单的答对题数，所以官方不公布"错几道能过"。想按答对题数大概估一下，用 [JLPT 估分器](/jlpt-score-calculator/)。
:::
:::en
Every level is scored out of 180. To pass you need **the total pass mark and the minimum in every section** — a high total doesn't help if one section falls below its minimum.

| Level | Pass mark | Sections | Section minimum |
|------|------|------|------|
| N1 | 100 / 180 | Language knowledge 60, Reading 60, Listening 60 | 19 each |
| N2 | 90 / 180 | Language knowledge 60, Reading 60, Listening 60 | 19 each |
| N3 | 95 / 180 | Language knowledge 60, Reading 60, Listening 60 | 19 each |
| N4 | 90 / 180 | Language knowledge & reading 120, Listening 60 | 38 and 19 |
| N5 | 80 / 180 | Language knowledge & reading 120, Listening 60 | 38 and 19 |

Scores are scaled for item difficulty, not a plain count of right answers, so the JLPT doesn't publish "how many you can miss". For a rough estimate from the number you got right, use the [JLPT score calculator](/jlpt-score-calculator/).
:::

## 什么时候考、怎么报名||Test Dates and Registration

:::zh
- **考试日期**：每年 7 月和 12 月，一般是当月第一个星期日。海外有些城市一年只考一次（只在 7 月或只在 12 月）。2026 年 12 月的考试是 **12 月 6 日**，考前准备见 [12 月考前指南](/jlpt-december-2026/)。
- **在日本考**：在 JEES 的官方报名网站 **MyJLPT** 上网报名，7 月考试一般 3 月到 4 月报名，12 月考试一般 8 月到 9 月报名。
- **在中国大陆考**：在教育部教育考试院的 JLPT 报名网站报名。
- **在其他国家和地区考**：通过当地的主办机构报名，各地机构名单在 [jlpt.jp](https://www.jlpt.jp/) 上能查到。热门城市的考场经常很快报满，开放报名后尽早报。

报名时间和费用每个国家都不一样，以当地主办机构的公告为准。
:::
:::en
- **Test dates**: every July and December, usually the first Sunday of the month. Some cities outside Japan hold it only once a year (July only or December only). The December 2026 test is on **6 December**; for the run-up, see the [December exam guide](/jlpt-december-2026/).
- **In Japan**: apply online on JEES's official site **MyJLPT** — usually March–April for the July test and August–September for December.
- **In mainland China**: apply on the JLPT registration site of the National Education Examinations Authority.
- **Elsewhere**: apply through your local host institution; the list is on [jlpt.jp](https://www.jlpt.jp/). Popular test centres fill up fast, so register as soon as applications open.

Dates and fees differ by country — your local host institution's notice is what counts.
:::

## 成绩和证书||Results and Certificates

:::zh
- **查成绩**：12 月考试的成绩一般在第二年 1 月底到 2 月初可以上网查（2025 年 12 月那次是 2026 年 1 月 30 日开放），7 月考试的成绩在 8 月底到 9 月初。
- **成绩单和证书**：日本国内考生的成绩单 2 月上旬寄出，海外考生由当地主办机构发放，一般在 3 月上旬前后。合格的考生同时拿到合格证书。
- **有效期**：JLPT 成绩和证书**本身没有有效期**。不过有的学校、公司会要求近几年的成绩，用之前先问清楚。
:::
:::en
- **Results online**: for the December test, usually from the end of January to early February (the December 2025 results opened on 30 January 2026); for the July test, late August to early September.
- **Score report and certificate**: in Japan the score report is mailed in early February; overseas it comes through the local host institution, usually around early March. If you pass, you get a certificate too.
- **Validity**: JLPT results and certificates **don't expire**. Some schools and employers do ask for a recent result, so check before you rely on an old one.
:::

## 我该考哪一级？||Which Level Should I Take?

:::zh
官方不再公布"学多少小时到哪一级"，最实际的办法是**直接做一组那个级别的题**：

1. 打开你想考的那一级的[练习题](/n3-practice/)，做一组 10 道的今日练习（每题有解析）。
2. 正确率稳定在七成左右，这一级就差不多了；明显低于一半，先往下一级打基础。
3. 离考试一个月左右，做一套那一级的[计时模拟题](/mock-test/)，看哪一科离单科最低分最近，最后的时间就花在那一科上。

多数人卡在两个地方：一是**听力**（读得懂的句子换成听就跟不上），二是**阅读时间不够**。这两样越早开始练越好。
:::
:::en
The JLPT no longer publishes study-hour estimates per level. The practical way to decide is to **do a set at that level**:

1. Open the [practice questions](/n3-practice/) for the level you have in mind and do a 10-question daily set (every question is explained).
2. If you're getting around 70% consistently, that level is within reach; well under half, build up at the level below first.
3. About a month before the test, take a [timed mock test](/mock-test/) at that level and see which section is closest to its minimum — that's where your last weeks should go.

Most people get stuck in one of two places: **listening** (sentences you can read are too fast to follow when spoken) and **running out of time in reading**. Start on both early.
:::

## 常见问题||FAQ

### JLPT 考口语和写作吗？||Does the JLPT test speaking or writing?

:::zh
不考。全部是四选一（听力有的是三选一）的选择题，只考读和听。
:::
:::en
No. Every question is multiple choice (four options; some listening items have three), testing reading and listening only.
:::

### 一年能考几次？||How many times a year is it held?

:::zh
两次，7 月和 12 月。同一次考试只能报一个级别。
:::
:::en
Twice, in July and December. You can sit only one level per test date.
:::

### N2 和 N1 有什么用？||What are N2 and N1 good for?

:::zh
在日本找工作、读大学和研究生时，N2 常常是最低要求，N1 更有竞争力；日本的部分签证积分制度也会给 N1、N2 加分。具体要求以学校、公司和相关机构为准。
:::
:::en
For jobs and university or graduate study in Japan, N2 is often the minimum and N1 stands out; Japan's points-based visa system also gives points for N1 and N2. Check the exact requirement with the school, employer or authority.
:::
