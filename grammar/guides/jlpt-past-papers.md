# JLPT 真题与官方样题||JLPT Past Papers (N5–N1)

:::zh
**先说结论：JLPT 官方不免费公开完整的历年真题试卷**，网上流传的"真题 PDF 合集"大多是扫描盗版。但官方网站（jlpt.jp）免费提供三套**官方样题**，其中两套的题目**直接选自 2010 年改革以后的真实考试**，带答案、听力录音稿和录音，N5 到 N1 都有。这一页把这些官方文件按级别整理成直接链接，再说明怎么用它们备考，以及本站能接着练什么：按考过次数整理的真题语法、计时模拟题和几千道按题型分类的练习题。
:::
:::en
**The short answer: the JLPT does not publish its full past test papers for free**, and most "JLPT past paper PDF" collections online are pirated scans. What the official site (jlpt.jp) does give away is three sets of **official sample questions** — two of them **taken from real tests held since the 2010 revision** — with answers, listening scripts and audio, for every level from N5 to N1. This page lists those official files level by level as direct links, explains how to use them, and what to practise next here: grammar from past tests ranked by how often it appeared, timed mock tests, and thousands of practice questions by item type.
:::

## 怎么用真题和样题||How to Use Past Questions

:::zh
1. **先计时做一遍。** 按正式考试的时间做（各级时长见 [12 月 JLPT 考前指南](/jlpt-december-2026/)），听力一段只放一遍。第一次做的成绩最能说明你现在的水平，之后再做同一套就只是复习了。
2. **对答案时分三类。** 不会的、会但做错的（看错题、时间不够）、蒙对的。蒙对的和做错的一样要复习。
3. **错题追到语法点和单词。** 每道错题找出考的是哪个语法、哪个词，回课文或单词表补上，比再做一套新题更有效。
4. **两套官方题做完，用模拟题补。** 官方免费的只有两套，建议一套留到考前一个月左右计时做，用来估计水平；做完以后用下面的模拟题和练习题继续练同样的题型。
:::
:::en
1. **Do it once, timed.** Use the real test's times (see the [December JLPT exam guide](/jlpt-december-2026/)) and play each listening item only once. Your first attempt is the honest measure; doing the same set again is only review.
2. **Sort your mistakes three ways:** didn't know, knew but got wrong (misread, ran out of time), and guessed right. Review the lucky guesses as if they were wrong.
3. **Trace every miss to a grammar point or word.** Going back to that lesson or word list does more than doing another new set.
4. **After the two official tests, top up with mock tests.** There are only two free official tests per level — keep one for about a month before the exam, timed, to gauge your level. After that, keep practising the same item types with the mock tests and practice questions below.
:::

## 常见问题||FAQ

:::zh
**哪里能下载 JLPT 真题？** 官方没有公开完整的历年试卷。免费的官方资料是上面三套样题，其中 2012 年版和 2018 年版选自 2010 年以后的真实考试。网上标着"真题"的完整 PDF 多半是盗版扫描，内容和版本都无法保证。

**官方样题够不够备考？** 不够当唯一的材料：每个级别只有两套完整的题。它最适合用来校准难度、熟悉题型和估计水平，再配合模拟题和按题型的练习。

**N4、N3 的旧题和现在的考试一样吗？** 2010 年改革后题型基本没变，所以 2012 年和 2018 年两套样题的题型和今天的考试一致；2010 年以前的旧试卷（旧 1–4 级）题型不同，不建议拿来练。
:::
:::en
**Where can I download JLPT past papers?** The JLPT doesn't publish full past tests. The free official material is the three sample sets above, and the 2012 and 2018 sets are taken from real tests given since 2010. Full "past paper" PDFs found elsewhere are usually pirated scans of unknown origin and version.

**Are the official samples enough to prepare?** Not as your only material: there are just two complete tests per level. They're best for calibrating the difficulty, learning the formats and gauging your level — then practise with mock tests and questions by item type.

**Are old N4/N3 papers like today's test?** The formats have barely changed since the 2010 revision, so the 2012 and 2018 sets match today's test. Papers from before 2010 (the old levels 1–4) use different formats and aren't worth practising with.
:::
