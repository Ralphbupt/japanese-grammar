# JLPT 阅读做题技巧||JLPT Reading Tips — How to Answer Each Question Type (N5–N1)

:::zh
JLPT 的阅读（読解）不是比谁读得最细，而是比谁能在有限时间里**找到答案所在的那一句**。这一页按题型讲做法：每个级别有哪些阅读题型、每篇大概能用几分钟、要不要先看问题、答案通常藏在哪里、常见的干扰项长什么样，最后是怎么用本站的阅读练习和模拟题来练。适用于 N5 到 N1。
:::
:::en
JLPT reading (読解) doesn't reward reading every word most carefully; it rewards **finding the one sentence the answer rests on** within the time you have. This page goes through it by question type: which reading types each level has, roughly how many minutes each passage can take, whether to read the question first, where the answer usually sits, what the usual wrong options look like, and how to practise with this site's reading drills and mock tests. It covers N5 to N1.
:::

## 时间预算：每篇几分钟||Time Budget per Passage

:::zh
阅读从不单独计时：N5–N3 和语法同一场，N2、N1 和文字・词汇・语法同一场。所以阅读能用多少时间，取决于前面的题做得多快。下面是一个参考分法（先把前面的题控制在左栏的时间里，剩下的给阅读）：

| 级别 | 这一场的时长 | 阅读前的部分 | 留给阅读 | 每篇参考用时 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| N5 | 40 分钟（语法・阅读） | 语法约 12–15 分钟 | 约 25 分钟 | 短文 2 篇 × 3 分钟，中文 1 篇 × 7 分钟，情報検索 1 篇 × 5 分钟 |
| N4 | 55 分钟（语法・阅读） | 语法约 15 分钟 | 约 35 分钟 | 短文 3 篇 × 3 分钟，中文 1 篇 × 8 分钟，情報検索 1 篇 × 8 分钟 |
| N3 | 70 分钟（语法・阅读） | 语法约 15–20 分钟 | 约 45–50 分钟 | 短文 4 篇 × 3 分钟，中文 2 篇 × 6 分钟，長文 1 篇 × 12 分钟，情報検索 1 篇 × 7 分钟 |
| N2 | 105 分钟（文字・词汇・语法・阅读） | 约 30–35 分钟 | 约 60–70 分钟 | 短文 5 篇 × 2–3 分钟，中文 3 篇 × 6 分钟，統合理解 7 分钟，主張理解 13 分钟，情報検索 6 分钟 |
| N1 | 110 分钟（文字・词汇・语法・阅读） | 约 30–35 分钟 | 约 70 分钟 | 短文 4 篇 × 3 分钟，中文 3 篇 × 7 分钟，長文 12 分钟，統合理解 8 分钟，主張理解 12 分钟，情報検索 6 分钟 |

几条原则：

- **N2、N1 先做什么由你决定，但别在词汇和语法上恋战。** 会的题几秒钟就选，不会的猜一个、做记号，时间留给阅读。
- **每篇设一个"截止时间"。** 到点还没确定就选最像的那个、做记号，往下走。一篇卡住拖垮后面三篇，是阅读最常见的失分方式。
- **情報検索放最后也可以。** 它在试卷的最后，不需要读懂全文，只要对条件，所以时间不够时它往往是最快能拿的分。但要确保至少留出 5 分钟给它。
- 用模拟题实际计时一次，把这张表改成你自己的节奏。
:::
:::en
Reading is never timed on its own: at N5–N3 it shares a sitting with grammar, at N2 and N1 with vocabulary and grammar too. So the time reading gets depends on how fast you finish what comes before it. A starting split (keep the earlier part inside the left-hand time and give the rest to reading):

| Level | The sitting | Before reading | Left for reading | Rough time per passage |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| N5 | 40 min (grammar & reading) | grammar about 12–15 min | about 25 min | short 2 × 3 min, mid 1 × 7 min, info 1 × 5 min |
| N4 | 55 min (grammar & reading) | grammar about 15 min | about 35 min | short 3 × 3 min, mid 1 × 8 min, info 1 × 8 min |
| N3 | 70 min (grammar & reading) | grammar about 15–20 min | about 45–50 min | short 4 × 3 min, mid 2 × 6 min, long 1 × 12 min, info 1 × 7 min |
| N2 | 105 min (vocabulary, grammar & reading) | about 30–35 min | about 60–70 min | short 5 × 2–3 min, mid 3 × 6 min, two texts 7 min, argument 13 min, info 6 min |
| N1 | 110 min (vocabulary, grammar & reading) | about 30–35 min | about 70 min | short 4 × 3 min, mid 3 × 7 min, long 12 min, two texts 8 min, argument 12 min, info 6 min |

A few rules:

- **At N2 and N1 the order is yours, but don't get stuck in vocabulary and grammar.** Answer what you know in seconds; for the rest, guess, mark it, and save the time for reading.
- **Give each passage a deadline.** When it passes and you still aren't sure, pick the likeliest option, mark it and move on. One stuck passage sinking the next three is the most common way to lose reading points.
- **Finding information can wait until last.** It's the final part of the paper and you don't need to understand the whole text, only check the conditions — so when time runs short it's often the quickest points left. Just make sure it gets at least 5 minutes.
- Time yourself on one mock test and turn this table into your own pace.
:::

## 先看问题还是先看文章？||Question First or Passage First?

:::zh
**先看问题，再读文章。** 大多数题型都适用：

- **短文・中文**：先扫一眼问题（不用看选项），知道要找"理由""作者的意见"还是"これ指的是什么"，再读文章，读到相关的地方停下来对选项。
- **中文、長文的多道题**：问题基本按文章顺序排列。第 1 题的答案在前面，最后一题常问全文主旨。可以读一段、做一题。
- **情報検索**：一定先看问题。问题里会写清"谁、什么条件"，文章（表格）只是查找对象，不需要从头读。
- **主張理解・統合理解**：先看问题确认问的是"A 和 B 的共同点""作者最想说什么"还是某个下划线，然后**通读全文**——主张要读完才知道，中途下结论容易被前半段的"一般看法"骗到。

选项最好**读完相关段落再看**。先看选项，脑子里会先装进四个说法，读文章时容易"认出"错误选项里的词。
:::
:::en
**Read the question first, then the passage.** It works for most types:

- **Short and mid-length passages**: glance at the question (not the options) so you know whether you're looking for a reason, the author's opinion, or what これ refers to; then read, and stop to check the options when you reach that part.
- **Several questions on one mid or long text**: the questions roughly follow the order of the text. The first one is answered near the start; the last one often asks about the whole text. You can read a paragraph, answer a question, and go on.
- **Finding information**: always the question first. It spells out who wants what under which conditions; the text (a table) is something to look things up in, not to read from the top.
- **Argument and two-text questions**: read the question to see whether it asks what A and B share, what the author most wants to say, or about an underlined part — then **read the whole text**. The argument only becomes clear at the end, and deciding halfway lets the "common view" in the first half fool you.

Look at the options **after** reading the relevant paragraph. Read them first and you go in with four claims in your head, and you'll "recognise" words from the wrong ones as you read.
:::

## 答案藏在哪里||Where the Answer Hides

:::zh
**1. 作者的主张在最后。** 日语议论文和随笔常见的结构是：先写一般的看法或经历，再用转折引出自己的意见，最后一段总结。问"筆者の言いたいことは何か""筆者の考えに合うのはどれか"时，先看最后一两句。常见的收尾句型：

- 〜のではないだろうか（难道不是……吗 → 作者就是这么认为）
- 〜べきだ／〜なければならない
- 〜と思う／〜と考える／〜たい
- 大切なのは〜ことだ

**2. 转折词之后是重点。** 「しかし」「ところが」「だが」「とはいえ」「にもかかわらず」后面才是作者真正想说的。前面的"一般认为……"往往正好是干扰项的来源。同样要注意「つまり」「要するに」「このように」，它们后面是总结，常常就是答案的另一种说法。

**3. 指示语看前面。** 问「『それ』とは何か」「『このこと』とはどういうことか」时，答案几乎总在下划线**紧前面**的一两句里。把你找到的内容代入下划线的位置读一遍，句子通顺就对了。

**4. "为什么"题找原因标志。** 「〜からだ」「〜ためだ」「〜ので」「〜のだ」常常就是理由所在。下划线题问「〜とあるが、なぜか」时，答案通常在下划线前后各一两句之内，而不是在文章另一头。

**5. 问"心情・感想"时找评价词。** 「うれしかった」「残念だ」「意外だった」「ほっとした」这类词所在的句子，往往就是答案的依据。
:::
:::en
**1. The author's point comes at the end.** Japanese essays and opinion pieces usually open with a common view or an experience, turn to the author's own opinion with a contrast word, and sum up in the last paragraph. When the question asks 筆者の言いたいことは何か (what the author wants to say) or 筆者の考えに合うのはどれか (which matches the author's view), read the last sentence or two first. Typical closing patterns:

- 〜のではないだろうか ("isn't it…?" — this is what the author believes)
- 〜べきだ / 〜なければならない (should / must)
- 〜と思う / 〜と考える / 〜たい (I think / I want)
- 大切なのは〜ことだ (what matters is…)

**2. What follows a contrast word is the point.** The author's real view comes after しかし, ところが, だが, とはいえ, にもかかわらず. The "people generally think…" before it is often exactly where the wrong options come from. Watch つまり, 要するに and このように too: what follows them is a summary, often the answer in other words.

**3. For a demonstrative, look just before it.** When the question is 「それ」とは何か or 「このこと」とはどういうことか, the answer is almost always in the sentence or two **right before** the underlined part. Put what you found in place of the underlined words and read it again; if the sentence still makes sense, you have it.

**4. For "why" questions, look for reason markers.** 〜からだ, 〜ためだ, 〜ので and 〜のだ often mark the reason. When an underlined question asks 〜とあるが、なぜか (why…?), the answer is usually within a sentence or two of the underline, not at the other end of the text.

**5. For feelings and impressions, find the evaluative words.** The sentence with うれしかった, 残念だ, 意外だった, ほっとした and the like is usually what the answer rests on.
:::

## 各题型的做法||Technique by Question Type

### 内容理解（短文）||Short Passages

:::zh
一篇只有一题，常见的问题是"这篇文章最想说什么"或"读完这封邮件，要做什么"。

- 通知、邮件类：答案几乎都是**一个行动**（什么时候、交什么、联系谁）。找「〜てください」「〜ようお願いします」「〜までに」这类句子。
- 随笔类：看最后一句，配合转折词。
- 时间短，读完还拿不准的不要回头重读全文，在两个最像的选项里选一个就走。
:::
:::en
One question per text, usually "what is this text mainly saying" or "after reading this email, what should you do".

- Notices and emails: the answer is nearly always **an action** (when, what to hand in, whom to contact). Look for 〜てください, 〜ようお願いします, 〜までに.
- Essays: read the last sentence, together with any contrast word.
- Time is short. If you're still unsure after one read, don't reread the whole thing — choose between the two likeliest options and move on.
:::

### 内容理解（中文・長文）||Mid-Length and Long Passages

:::zh
- 问题按文章顺序排，一段对一题，边读边做。
- 下划线题：答案在下划线附近（见上文"指示语""为什么"）。
- 最后一题常问全文主旨，这时回到最后一段，而不是根据某一个细节作答。
- N3、N1 的長文篇幅长，先读每段的第一句，大致知道每段讲什么，再按问题回到对应的段落细读。
:::
:::en
- The questions follow the text, roughly one per paragraph; answer as you read.
- Underlined questions: the answer is near the underline (see "demonstratives" and "why" above).
- The last question often asks about the whole text: go back to the final paragraph rather than answering from one detail.
- The 長文 at N3 and N1 is long: read the first sentence of each paragraph to see what each one is about, then go back to the right paragraph for each question.
:::

### 統合理解（N2・N1）||Comparing Two Texts (N2・N1)

:::zh
A、B 两篇写同一个话题（例如"孩子该不该有手机"），问题一般是：

- 「AとBの両方が述べていることは何か」：两篇的**共同点**
- 「AとBはどのような立場か」：两人的立场（都赞成、都反对、一赞一反、各有条件）

做法：读 A 时在旁边写一个字概括立场（赞成／反对／有条件），B 也一样，再看问题。问共同点时，只在 A 里出现、或只在 B 里出现的内容都是错的，哪怕它本身说得没错。
:::
:::en
Texts A and B take on the same topic (say, "should children have smartphones"). The questions are usually:

- AとBの両方が述べていることは何か — what **both** texts say
- AとBはどのような立場か — where each writer stands (both for, both against, one each way, each with conditions)

How: as you read A, jot one word for its stance (for / against / conditional), same for B, then turn to the question. When it asks what both say, anything found in only A or only B is wrong, even if it's true.
:::

### 主張理解（N2・N1）||The Author's Argument (N2・N1)

:::zh
一篇较长的议论文，3–4 题，最后一题几乎都问作者的主张。

- 先看问题，再**通读**。作者常先摆出一种常见看法，然后用「しかし」「確かに〜が、」推翻它。「確かに〜」后面那句是作者先承认的部分，不是结论。
- 读的时候把每段的作用在心里标一下：问题提出 → 一般看法 → 反驳 → 作者的主张 → 总结。
- 选项里常有"作者提到过、但不是主张"的说法，它们是最难排除的干扰项（见下一节）。
:::
:::en
One long argumentative essay with 3–4 questions; the last almost always asks for the author's claim.

- Read the questions, then **the whole text**. The author often sets up a common view and then knocks it down with しかし or 確かに〜が、. What follows 確かに is a concession, not the conclusion.
- As you read, note each paragraph's job: question raised → common view → counter-argument → the author's claim → summary.
- The options often include something the author mentions but doesn't argue for — the hardest wrong option to rule out (see the next section).
:::

### 情報検索（N5–N1）||Finding Information (N5–N1)

:::zh
一张广告、课程表、活动通知或价目表，问"这个人可以选哪一个"或"要付多少钱"。

- **先把问题里的条件逐条列出来**：例如"周末、上午、3000 日元以内、带孩子"。
- 拿条件去扫表格，**一条一条排除**，不要从头读表格。
- 注意表格下面的小字：「※」「ただし」「〜の方は」「〜以外」「割引」「要予約」。答案经常就靠这一行改变——比如"学生半价""周日休息""需要提前 3 天预约"。
- 算钱的题：确认是一人还是全家、含不含税、有没有折扣、几次。
:::
:::en
An ad, class timetable, event notice or price list; the question asks which option a person can choose, or how much they'll pay.

- **List the question's conditions first**, e.g. "weekend, morning, under ¥3,000, bringing a child".
- Scan the table against them and **rule out one condition at a time**; don't read the table from the top.
- Read the small print under the table: ※, ただし, 〜の方は, 〜以外, 割引, 要予約. The answer often turns on that one line — "half price for students", "closed on Sundays", "book three days ahead".
- Money questions: check whether it's per person or for the family, tax included or not, any discount, how many times.
:::

## 常见陷阱||Common Traps

:::zh
**1. 选项本身没错，但不是在问的东西。** 问"作者最想说什么"，选项却是文章里提到的一个事实或例子。它确实在文章里，但不是主张。先确认问题问的是"理由""主张""事实"还是"指代"，再判断选项。

**2. 选项重复原文的词，但意思变了。** 这是最常见的干扰项：词都对得上，可是

- 否定和肯定反了（「〜とは限らない」被改成"一定不……"）
- 程度变了（「〜こともある」被改成"总是……"，「少し」变成"大大地"）
- 主语换了（原文是"年轻人这么认为"，选项变成"作者这么认为"）
- 因果反了（原文"因为 A 所以 B"，选项"因为 B 所以 A"）

对选项时盯住这几个地方：**谁、肯定还是否定、程度、因果方向**。

**3. 只看了一半的句子。** 「〜わけではない」「〜というより」「〜だけでなく」这类表达会改变整句的意思。只看到前半句就选，往往正好选中干扰项。

**4. 用常识代替原文。** "选项说得很有道理"不是选它的理由。答案必须能在原文里找到依据；文章里没写的，再合理也不选。

**5. 选项说得太绝对。** 「必ず」「すべて」「決して」「〜しかない」这类绝对说法，如果原文没有这么强的语气，基本是错的。
:::
:::en
**1. The option is true, but it isn't what's asked.** The question asks what the author most wants to say, and the option is a fact or example from the text. It is in the text, but it isn't the argument. Check first whether the question asks for a reason, the claim, a fact or a reference, then judge the options.

**2. The option repeats the text's words but changes the meaning.** The most common trap: the words match, but

- negative and positive are swapped (〜とは限らない "not necessarily" becomes "never")
- the degree shifts (〜こともある "sometimes" becomes "always"; 少し "a little" becomes "greatly")
- the subject changes (the text says young people think so; the option says the author does)
- cause and effect are reversed (the text says A causes B; the option says B causes A)

When checking an option, watch these four: **who, positive or negative, how much, which way the cause runs**.

**3. Reading only half the sentence.** Expressions like 〜わけではない (it isn't that…), 〜というより (rather than…) and 〜だけでなく (not only…) change the meaning of the whole sentence. Choose after reading only the first half and you often pick exactly the trap.

**4. Common sense instead of the text.** "That option sounds reasonable" is no reason to choose it. The answer must have support in the text; if the text doesn't say it, however sensible it sounds, it's out.

**5. Options that are too absolute.** 必ず, すべて, 決して, 〜しかない — if the text isn't that strong, an option phrased this way is almost always wrong.
:::

## 怎么练||How to Practise

:::zh
1. **按题型练，再整套练。** 本站的阅读练习按级别分页，可以只练某一个题型：[N5 阅读](/n5-reading/)・[N4 阅读](/n4-reading/)・[N3 阅读](/n3-reading/)・[N2 阅读](/n2-reading/)・[N1 阅读](/n1-reading/)。
2. **每篇都计时。** 按上面的"每篇参考用时"练，超时就停，记下来。
3. **做完找"答案句"。** 对完答案后，在原文里找出决定答案的那一句；错题要说清楚自己选的选项错在哪（谁、否定、程度、因果中的哪一个）。题目下面的 🤖 AI 讲解可以帮你逐句看。
4. **用模拟题练时间分配。** 完整计时做一套，看阅读最后剩几分钟：[N5 模拟题](/n5-mock-test/)・[N4 模拟题](/n4-mock-test/)・[N3 模拟题](/n3-mock-test/)・[N2 模拟题](/n2-mock-test/)・[N1 模拟题](/n1-mock-test/)。
5. **语法是阅读的地基。** 读不懂一句话，常常是卡在一个语法上（〜わけではない、〜ざるを得ない……）。按级别查 [N5](/N5_grammar_list/)・[N4](/N4_grammar_list/)・[N3](/N3_grammar_list/)・[N2](/N2_grammar_list/)・[N1](/N1_grammar_list/) 语法清单。
:::
:::en
1. **Practise by type, then whole tests.** This site's reading drills have a page per level, and you can drill a single type: [N5 reading](/n5-reading/), [N4 reading](/n4-reading/), [N3 reading](/n3-reading/), [N2 reading](/n2-reading/), [N1 reading](/n1-reading/).
2. **Time every passage.** Use the per-passage times above; stop when time's up and note it.
3. **Find the answer sentence afterwards.** After checking, find the sentence in the text that decides the answer; for a wrong answer, say exactly what was wrong with your choice (who, negative, degree or cause). The 🤖 AI explanation under each question can walk through it sentence by sentence.
4. **Practise your time split on mock tests.** Do a full timed test and see how many minutes reading has left: [N5 mock test](/n5-mock-test/), [N4 mock test](/n4-mock-test/), [N3 mock test](/n3-mock-test/), [N2 mock test](/n2-mock-test/), [N1 mock test](/n1-mock-test/).
5. **Grammar is what reading stands on.** A sentence you can't follow often hinges on one grammar point (〜わけではない, 〜ざるを得ない …). Look it up in the [N5](/N5_grammar_list/), [N4](/N4_grammar_list/), [N3](/N3_grammar_list/), [N2](/N2_grammar_list/) or [N1](/N1_grammar_list/) grammar list.
