# JLPT N5 历年真题语法||JLPT N5 Grammar from Past Tests (2012–2025)

:::zh
本站收集到 2012–2025 年间 7 场 JLPT N5 真题（不是每一场都有，场次见文末说明），逐场统计了语法题，一共考过 **45 个语法**。下面按考过的次数排列，每个都写了接续、意思、考过哪几次，并链接到本站的讲解。其中 **9 个考过 3 次以上**，最值得先看；另外还有 **12 个其他考点**（助词用法、接续词、动词变形、副词等），列在后面单独一张表里。

用法建议：先把「考过 3 次以上」的过一遍，点进讲解看例句；再到 [N5 练习题](/n5-practice/) 做题，题目考到的语法如果在真题里出现过，答题后会标出「真题考过几次」。
:::
:::en
We have 7 real JLPT N5 tests from 2012–2025 (not every sitting; the list is in the notes) and went through their grammar items: **45 grammar patterns** came up. They're listed by how often each was tested, with its form, meaning, the sittings it appeared in, and a link to the lesson that teaches it. **9 came up three times or more** — start there. There are also **12 other test points** (particle uses, conjunctions, conjugation forms, adverbs and the like) in a separate table after them.

How to use it: go through the patterns tested 3+ times first and open their lessons for examples; then drill in [N5 practice](/n5-practice/) — a question on a pattern from this list shows how often the real test asked it.
:::
