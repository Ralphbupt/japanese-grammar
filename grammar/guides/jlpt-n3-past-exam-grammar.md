# JLPT N3 历年真题语法||JLPT N3 Grammar from Past Tests (2010–2026)

:::zh
本站逐场统计了 2010–2026 年 31 场 JLPT N3 真题的语法题，一共考过 **145 个语法**。下面按考过的次数排列，每个都写了接续、意思、考过哪几次，并链接到本站的讲解。其中 **83 个考过 3 次以上**，最值得先看；而且有 **73 个其实是 N4 及以下的语法**——N3 并不只考 N3 语法，N4 的基础不牢，语法题照样丢分。

用法建议：先把「考过 3 次以上」的过一遍，点进讲解看例句；再到 [N3 练习题](/n3-practice/) 做题，题目考到的语法如果在真题里出现过，答题后会标出「真题考过几次」。
:::
:::en
We went through the grammar items of all 31 real JLPT N3 tests from 2010 to 2026: **145 grammar patterns** came up. They're listed by how often each was tested, with its form, meaning, the sittings it appeared in, and a link to the lesson that teaches it. **83 came up three times or more** — start there. And **73 are N4-or-lower grammar**: N3 doesn't only test N3 patterns, so gaps in N4 cost points too.

How to use it: go through the patterns tested 3+ times first and open their lessons for examples; then drill in [N3 practice](/n3-practice/) — a question on a pattern from this list shows how often the real test asked it.
:::

## 说明||Notes

:::zh
- 年份是 7 月（第 1 回）和 12 月（第 2 回）考试，2020 年 7 月因疫情停考。
- 本站逐场看了2010–2026 年 31 场 N3 真题的语法部分（在「言语知识（语法）・阅读」那一场）（問題 1 语法形式判断、問題 2 句子组合、問題 3 文章语法），按答案判断每道题考的语法再统计。只算语法题本身的考点，阅读、听力里出现的不算；一道题考的是单纯的时态、接续词等、说不出具体句型的，不列入。
- 一个语法在同一场里考了两次，只算一次；同一个语法的不同形式合在一起算。判断难免有出入，发现错误欢迎留言。
- 意思和接续由本站编写；不收录真题原题（试题版权属于国际交流基金和日本国际教育支援协会）。
:::
:::en
- Sittings are the July (1st) and December (2nd) tests; July 2020 was cancelled.
- We went through the grammar section of all 31 N3 papers from 2010 to 2026 (in the grammar and reading booklet) (問題1 grammar form, 問題2 sentence composition, 問題3 text grammar) and, using the answers, noted the pattern each item tests. Only the point of a grammar item counts, not patterns that appear in reading or listening; items testing plain tense, a connective or nothing nameable are left out.
- A pattern tested twice in one sitting counts once; forms of one pattern count together. Some calls are judgment calls — if you spot a mistake, please leave a comment.
- Meanings and forms are written for this site; no exam questions are reproduced (the papers are copyright of the Japan Foundation and JEES).
