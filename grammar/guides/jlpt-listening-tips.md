# JLPT 听力做题技巧||JLPT Listening Tips (N5–N1)

:::zh
JLPT 的听力（聴解）每段录音**只放一遍**，不能回头再听。很多人听力丢分不是因为完全听不懂，而是不知道每种题型"该听哪一句"：答案藏在被修正之后的那句话里，干扰项却是对话里明明说过的内容。这一页按题型讲做题方法：播放前看什么、听的时候抓什么、常见的陷阱，以及笔记和题与题之间的时间怎么用。最后是怎么用本站的听力练习和模拟题来练。
:::
:::en
In the JLPT listening section (聴解) every recording is played **only once** — there is no going back. Most lost points don't come from understanding nothing; they come from not knowing which sentence each item type is really testing: the answer sits in the line after a correction, while the wrong options are things the speakers really did say. This page goes through the techniques item type by item type: what to look at before the audio, what to listen for, the usual traps, and how to use notes and the gaps between items. At the end: how to practise with this site's listening questions and mock tests.
:::

## 各级别的题型||Item Types by Level

:::zh
| 题型 | 考什么 | 级别 | 题本上印什么 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 課題理解 | 接下来要做什么、买什么、带什么 | N5–N1 | N5/N4 多为图；N3 起多为文字 |
| ポイント理解 | 理由、哪一个、什么时候 | N5–N1 | N5/N4 多为图；N3 起为文字 |
| 概要理解 | 整段话的主旨、说话人的看法 | N3–N1 | 什么都不印 |
| 発話表現 | 看图，这个场景该说什么 | N5–N3 | 一张图，箭头指着说话的人 |
| 即時応答 | 听一句话，选最自然的回应 | N5–N1 | 什么都不印 |
| 統合理解 | 综合几个人的说法或一段说明，做出判断 | N2–N1 | 第 1 题不印；后面的题可能印选项 |

听力时长：N5 30 分钟、N4 35 分钟、N3 40 分钟、N2 50 分钟、N1 55 分钟（实际会因录音略有出入）。听力单独计分，满分 60，**低于 19 分就算总分够也不合格**（详见 [12 月 JLPT 考前指南](/jlpt-december-2026/)）。
:::
:::en
| Item type | What it tests | Levels | What the booklet shows |
|------|------|------|------|
| 課題理解 (task-based comprehension) | what someone will do, buy or bring next | N5–N1 | mostly pictures at N5/N4; mostly text from N3 |
| ポイント理解 (point comprehension) | a reason, which one, when | N5–N1 | mostly pictures at N5/N4; text from N3 |
| 概要理解 (summary comprehension) | the main point, the speaker's opinion | N3–N1 | nothing |
| 発話表現 (verbal expressions) | what to say in the pictured situation | N5–N3 | one picture, an arrow pointing at the speaker |
| 即時応答 (quick response) | the most natural reply to one line | N5–N1 | nothing |
| 統合理解 (integrated comprehension) | a judgement combining several speakers or an explanation | N2–N1 | nothing for item 1; later items may print options |

Listening time: N5 30 min, N4 35, N3 40, N2 50, N1 55 (it varies slightly with the recordings). Listening is scored on its own out of 60, and **below 19 is a fail even with a passing total** (see the [December JLPT exam guide](/jlpt-december-2026/)).
:::

## 通用原则||General Rules

:::zh
- **只放一遍，不要回想上一题。** 没听懂就马上猜一个、涂上，把注意力放回下一题。为上一题懊恼的那几秒，正好是下一题的场景说明。
- **先听"谁在问、问的是谁"。** 题目开头会说「男の人と女の人が話しています」，问题会说「女の人は…」。问的是女的要做什么，男的说的计划就是干扰项。
- **转折词后面才是重点。** 「でも」「ただ」「実は」「〜けど」「やっぱり」「それより」后面的那句，比前面的更可能是答案。
- **选项没有"听到过"就对的。** 干扰项几乎都是对话里真的出现过的词。判断标准不是"听到了"，而是"最后有没有被否定、是不是问题问的那一点"。
- **可以记笔记。** 题本上可以写。只记关键词、数字和"×"（被否定的候选），不要写完整句子。
:::
:::en
- **It plays once — don't replay the last item in your head.** If you missed it, guess immediately, mark it, and turn your attention to the next one. The seconds you spend regretting the last item are exactly when the next item's setting is read out.
- **Catch who is speaking and who the question is about.** Each item opens with something like 「男の人と女の人が話しています」 and the question says 「女の人は…」. If it asks what the woman will do, the man's plans are the distractors.
- **What comes after a turn word matters most.** The line after 「でも」, 「ただ」, 「実は」, 「〜けど」, 「やっぱり」 or 「それより」 is more likely to hold the answer than the line before it.
- **An option isn't right just because you heard it.** Distractors are almost always words that really came up in the dialogue. The test isn't "did I hear it?" but "was it rejected in the end, and is it the point the question asks about?"
- **You may take notes.** You can write on the question booklet. Note only key words, numbers and an "×" next to rejected candidates — never whole sentences.
:::

## 課題理解：最后的决定才算数||課題理解: The Last Decision Wins

:::zh
**播放前**：先扫一眼选项（N5/N4 是图，N3 起是文字），看出它们是同一类东西（四样东西、四个时间、四个动作组合），心里有个框架。

**听什么**：开头会先告诉你问题，例如「女の人はこれから何をしますか」，对话结束后再说一遍。对话里通常会提到三四个候选，然后一个一个被排除。最常见的排除方式：

| 排除方式 | 典型说法 |
|------|------|
| 已经做了 / 已经有了 | 「それはもう買いました」「まだたくさんあります」 |
| 别人负责 | 「お茶は私が持っていきます」 |
| 改到以后 | 「それは来週でいいです」 |
| 计划变了 | 「あ、やっぱりそっちを先にお願い」 |

**技巧**：问题是「これから」「まず」「最初に」的时候，要找的是**接下来第一件**要做的事，不是全部要做的事。N3 起会出需要再推一步的题，例如约好的时间、路上要多久，再加一句「少し早めに出たほうがいい」——答案是算出来的时间，不是对话里直接说出的那个。N5 常考数字和顺序（第几页、第几题、几点、几个），数字一出现就写下来。
:::
:::en
**Before the audio**: glance at the options (pictures at N5/N4, text from N3) and see what kind of thing they are — four objects, four times, four combinations of actions — so you have a frame ready.

**What to listen for**: the question comes first, e.g. 「女の人はこれから何をしますか」, and is repeated after the dialogue. The dialogue usually mentions three or four candidates and then rules them out one by one. The usual ways they're ruled out:

| How it's ruled out | Typical line |
|------|------|
| Already done / already have it | 「それはもう買いました」「まだたくさんあります」 |
| Someone else will do it | 「お茶は私が持っていきます」 |
| Put off until later | 「それは来週でいいです」 |
| Change of plan | 「あ、やっぱりそっちを先にお願い」 |

**Technique**: when the question says 「これから」, 「まず」 or 「最初に」, you want the **first** thing to be done next, not everything on the list. From N3 some items need one more step: the agreed time, how long the trip takes, then 「少し早めに出たほうがいい」 — the answer is the time you work out, not the one said aloud. N5 likes numbers and order (which page, which question, what time, how many); write each number down the moment you hear it.
:::

## ポイント理解：先知道问题，专听理由||ポイント理解: You Know the Question — Listen for the Reason

:::zh
**播放前**：问题在对话之前就会告诉你（对话后再说一遍）。N3 起选项印成文字，N2/N1 在播放对话前会留时间读选项。利用这段时间把四个选项的**区别**找出来——它们往往只差一个点。

**听什么**：这类题最常问「どうして〜のですか」（为什么）、「何が一番〜ですか」（什么最…）。最常见的套路是**表面理由被否定**：对方先猜一个理由，说话人否认，再说出真正的原因：

- 「忙しかったんですか。」→「いえ、そうじゃなくて、**実は**…」
- 「値段が高いからですか。」→「それもあるけど、**一番は**…」

听到「そうじゃなくて」「というより」「一番は」「実は」，后面那句就是答案。

**陷阱**：四个选项通常都在对话里出现过，只是**不是问题问的那一点**。问"为什么不去"，选项里会有"去了以后要做什么"；问"最担心什么"，选项里会有"已经解决了的问题"。N5/N4 的认人题（どの人ですか）靠帽子、眼镜、站的位置一句一句排除，每听到一个特征就在图上划掉不符合的人。
:::
:::en
**Before the audio**: you hear the question before the dialogue (and again after it). From N3 the options are printed as text, and at N2/N1 there is time to read them before the dialogue starts. Use it to spot how the four options **differ** — often only in one point.

**What to listen for**: the classic questions are 「どうして〜のですか」 (why) and 「何が一番〜ですか」 (what is most …). The most common pattern is **a surface reason that gets denied**: the other person guesses a reason, the speaker says no, then gives the real one:

- 「忙しかったんですか。」→「いえ、そうじゃなくて、**実は**…」
- 「値段が高いからですか。」→「それもあるけど、**一番は**…」

When you hear 「そうじゃなくて」, 「というより」, 「一番は」 or 「実は」, the next sentence is the answer.

**Trap**: all four options usually do come up in the dialogue — they're just **not the point being asked about**. Ask "why didn't they go?" and one option will be what they'll do once they get there; ask "what worries them most?" and one option will be a problem already solved. In the N5/N4 "which person?" items (どの人ですか), people are told apart by hat, glasses or where they stand; cross out everyone who doesn't fit as each detail comes.
:::

## 概要理解：听整体，不抓细节||概要理解: Listen for the Whole, Not the Details

:::zh
**播放前**：题本上什么都没有，问题也**只在最后**才说。所以你不知道会问什么，只能听整体。

**听什么**：通常是一个人说一段（广播、讲课、店员介绍）或两三个人聊天，最后问「何について話していますか」（在谈什么）或「〜はどう考えていますか」（怎么看）。要抓的是：
- **开头和结尾**——主题和结论通常在这两处；
- **说话人的态度**——赞成还是反对、满意还是不满。「〜とは思いますが」「確かに〜。でも…」这种先让步再转折的说法，后半句才是真正的看法。

**陷阱**：只覆盖一部分内容的选项、把细节当成主题的选项、态度说反的选项。笔记只写"话题 + 态度（○/×）"，听完四个读出的选项各打一个记号。
:::
:::en
**Before the audio**: the booklet shows nothing, and the question comes **only at the end**. You don't know what will be asked, so you have to listen to the whole.

**What to listen for**: usually one speaker (an announcement, a lecture, a shop assistant explaining something) or two or three people talking, followed by 「何について話していますか」 (what are they talking about) or 「〜はどう考えていますか」 (what does … think). What to catch:
- **The beginning and the end** — the topic and the conclusion usually sit there;
- **The speaker's attitude** — for or against, satisfied or not. With a concede-then-turn line like 「〜とは思いますが」 or 「確かに〜。でも…」, the second half is the real opinion.

**Traps**: options that cover only part of the talk, options that turn a detail into the topic, and options with the attitude reversed. Note only "topic + attitude (○/×)", then mark each of the four options as it is read out.
:::

## 発話表現：看箭头，想立场（N5–N3）||発話表現: Check the Arrow, Think About Who Speaks (N5–N3)

:::zh
**播放前**：先看图——箭头指着谁、在什么地方（店里、公司、别人家门口）、这个人想做什么。

**听什么**：录音先说场景，然后问「何と言いますか」，读出 3 个回应（不印在题本上）。三个选项都是"这种场合可能听到的话"，错的那两个是：
- **立场颠倒**：该请求别人的时候，选项是自己允许别人（「どうぞ使ってください」 vs 「使ってもいいですか」）；
- **时机不对**：进门的时候说出门的话（「いってきます」 vs 「ただいま」）；
- **是对方才说的话**：店员才会说「いらっしゃいませ」。

**技巧**：读完图先自己想一句"如果我是箭头指的人，我会说什么"，再听选项对照。
:::
:::en
**Before the audio**: look at the picture first — who the arrow points to, where they are (a shop, an office, someone's front door), and what they want to do.

**What to listen for**: the recording describes the situation, asks 「何と言いますか」, and reads three replies (not printed). All three are things you might hear in that kind of place; the two wrong ones are:
- **Roles reversed**: the person should ask permission, but the option gives it (「どうぞ使ってください」 vs 「使ってもいいですか」);
- **Wrong moment**: a leaving phrase on arrival (「いってきます」 vs 「ただいま」);
- **The other person's line**: only the shop assistant says 「いらっしゃいませ」.

**Technique**: after looking at the picture, think of what you would say if you were the person the arrow points to, then compare with the options as you hear them.
:::

## 即時応答：选项不印，三秒定答案||即時応答: Nothing Printed, Decide in Seconds

:::zh
**播放前**：没有可看的东西。题本上只有答题栏，选项**不印**，只听一遍。

**听什么**：一句话，接着 3 个回应。听第一句时先判断它的**意图**——是在问、在请求、在邀请、在道歉还是在抱怨？意图听对了，三个回应里一般只剩一个合适。

**陷阱**：
- **重复关键词**：错误选项照搬原句里的词，听起来相关，其实答非所问；
- **字面意思和实际意思不同**：「時間ありますか」问的是有没有空，不是现在几点；「ちょっと…」常常是委婉拒绝；
- **时态和视角不对**：问"明天能来吗"，回答"昨天去了"；
- **固定说法用错时机**（N5/N4 高频）：「お先に失礼します」→「お疲れさまでした」，「いただきます」不能回答「ごちそうさまでした」；
- **N2/N1 的口语**：「〜っけ」「〜てもらえない？」「〜しっぱなし」「〜といて」，要听出关系（上司/同事/朋友）和语气。

**技巧**：三个选项边听边在心里标 ○/×/△，第三个读完立刻涂。短题之间的作答时间很短（几秒），不要等。
:::
:::en
**Before the audio**: there's nothing to look at. The booklet has only the answer row — the options are **not printed**, and you hear them once.

**What to listen for**: one line, then three replies. While the first line plays, work out its **intent** — is it asking, requesting, inviting, apologising, complaining? Get the intent right and usually only one of the three replies still fits.

**Traps**:
- **Repeated key words**: a wrong option reuses a word from the line, so it sounds related but doesn't answer it;
- **Literal vs real meaning**: 「時間ありますか」 asks if you're free, not what time it is; 「ちょっと…」 is often a polite no;
- **Wrong tense or viewpoint**: asked "can you come tomorrow?", the option says "I went yesterday";
- **Set phrases at the wrong moment** (very common at N5/N4): 「お先に失礼します」→「お疲れさまでした」; you don't answer 「いただきます」 with 「ごちそうさまでした」;
- **N2/N1 casual speech**: 「〜っけ」, 「〜てもらえない？」, 「〜しっぱなし」, 「〜といて」 — hear the relationship (boss, colleague, friend) and the tone.

**Technique**: mark each reply ○/×/△ in your head as it plays and fill in the answer as soon as the third one ends. The answering time between short items is only a few seconds — don't wait.
:::

## 統合理解：记表格（N2・N1）||統合理解: Take Notes in a Grid (N2・N1)

:::zh
**第 1 题**：三个人讨论一件事，最后做决定，问"决定了什么"。题本上不印选项，问题在最后才说。常见的套路：第一个人提出方案，第二个人反对，**真正让当事人拿定主意的是另一个人的说法**。所以要盯着当事人最后一句「じゃあ、〜にします」「それなら〜かな」。

**后面的题**：先听一段说明（例如店员介绍四种商品、讲解四个方案），再听两个人讨论各自选哪个，分别问两个人的选择。这是**最需要笔记**的题：
- 说明部分边听边记一张小表——四个选项各写一两个关键词（价格、时间、特点）；
- 讨论部分在每个人的条件旁边写"男/女"，例如"女：安い×　近い○"；
- 最后对照表格，两个人可能选同一个，也可能不同，不要因为第一问选了 A 就默认第二问不是 A。
:::
:::en
**Item 1**: three people discuss something and reach a decision; the question asks what was decided. No options are printed and the question comes only at the end. The usual pattern: one person proposes, a second objects, and **what actually makes the decision-maker choose is someone else's point**. So listen hard for the decision-maker's last line: 「じゃあ、〜にします」, 「それなら〜かな」.

**The later items**: first an explanation (a shop assistant describing four products, someone outlining four plans), then two people discussing which one each will pick, with a separate question about each person's choice. These need **notes more than any other item**:
- During the explanation, jot a small grid — one or two key words per option (price, time, feature);
- During the discussion, write "M/F" next to each person's conditions, e.g. "F: cheap ×, close ○";
- Then match against the grid. The two people may pick the same option or different ones — don't assume the second answer isn't A because the first one was.
:::

## 题与题之间的时间||Using the Gaps Between Items

:::zh
真题里，长一点的题（課題理解、ポイント理解、概要理解、統合理解）答完后留大约十几秒作答，短题（発話表現、即時応答）只有几秒。

- **长题**：作答时间里先涂答案，剩下的几秒**提前看下一题的选项或图**，这比反复纠结上一题有用得多；
- **短题**：听完第三个选项就涂，然后马上准备听下一句；
- **大题之间**会有例题和说明，已经熟悉题型的话，可以用这段时间预览后面几题的选项；
- 答题卡**当场涂**，不要先在题本上做记号、最后再统一涂——听力结束后不会给你额外的时间。
:::
:::en
In the real test, the longer items (課題理解, ポイント理解, 概要理解, 統合理解) are followed by roughly a dozen-plus seconds to answer; the short ones (発話表現, 即時応答) by only a few.

- **Long items**: fill in the answer first, then use the remaining seconds to **look ahead at the next item's options or picture** — far more useful than agonising over the last one;
- **Short items**: fill in the answer when the third reply ends, then get ready for the next line;
- **Between parts** there's a worked example and instructions; if you already know the format, use that time to preview the options of the next few items;
- Fill in the answer sheet **as you go**, not in the booklet first with the plan to transfer later — there's no extra time once the listening ends.
:::

## 怎么练||How to Practise

:::zh
1. **按题型练**：在各级别的听力练习里先集中做一种题型，直到能说出"这类题该听哪一句"。每题答完可以看原文（带振假名）和翻译、逐句重播，没听懂的那句单独反复听。
2. **复盘错题**：每道错题问自己三个问题——我是没听懂、听懂了但被干扰项骗了、还是问题问的人/点搞错了？后两种靠技巧就能改。
3. **听写关键句**：把答案所在的那一句跟着录音写下来，尤其是转折词后面的那句。
4. **严格计时做模拟题**：模拟题的听力部分整套连续播放、只放一遍、题与题之间留作答时间，和考试节奏一样。考前至少做一两套。

:::en
1. **Practise one item type at a time**: in your level's listening practice, stick to one type until you can say which sentence that type is really testing. After each item you can read the script (with furigana) and the translation and replay it line by line — loop the line you missed.
2. **Review every mistake**: ask yourself which it was — I didn't understand it, I understood but fell for a distractor, or I got the person or the point of the question wrong? The last two are fixed by technique alone.
3. **Dictate the key line**: write down the sentence that holds the answer while replaying it, especially the line after a turn word.
4. **Do mock tests under real timing**: the mock tests play the whole listening section straight through, once, with answering time after each item, just like the exam. Do at least one or two before the test.
