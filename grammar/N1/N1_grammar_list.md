# N1 文法チェックリスト||N1 Grammar Checklist

:::zh
N1 全部约 100 个语法点的清单。N1 是 JLPT 的最高级别，考的是报纸、社论、公文、致辞和小说里的高阶书面表达——这份表按课的顺序覆盖时间、限定范围、例示并列、强调极限、逆接让步、条件假定、目的原因、评价判断、感情、样子状态、文语等全部考点。
:::

:::en
A full cheatsheet of ~100 N1 grammar points. N1 is the top JLPT level, testing the advanced written Japanese of newspapers, editorials, official documents, speeches and fiction — listed in lesson order, covering time, limits and scope, examples and pairs, emphasis, concession, conditions, purpose and cause, evaluation, emotion, manner, classical forms and more.
:::

:::zh
> JLPT N1 核心语法约 100 条。书面语、文语色彩浓，固定搭配多，考试重点在接续、后项限制和近义语法的区别。
:::

:::en
> About 100 core N1 grammar points. Heavily written and classical in flavour, full of fixed collocations — the exam tests how each one connects, what can follow it, and how it differs from its near-synonyms.
:::
